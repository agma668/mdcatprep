# 📊 PATTERN ANALYSIS & STATISTICS

## Test Structure (Consistent 2012–2024)
- **Total MCQs:** 100 (split into 2 sections)
- **Section 1 — Science Achievement:** ~50 MCQs (Biology ~20, Chemistry ~20, Physics ~10-15)
- **Section 2 — Science & Math Reasoning:** ~50 MCQs (Science Reasoning ~25, Math Reasoning ~25)
- **Duration:** 2 hours (120 minutes)
- **Negative Marking:** -0.25 for Bio, Chem, Physics ONLY | No negative marking in Reasoning
