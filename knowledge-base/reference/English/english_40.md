# MDCAT English MCQs — Source: .com (40 questions with answers & explanations)

## Formal and Lexical Aspects

### Q1. Convert to passive voice: 'The cat chased the mouse.'
A. The mouse was chased by the cat.
B. The mouse is chasing the cat.
C. The cat was chased by the mouse.
D. The mouse chases the cat.
**Answer: A** — Simple past active becomes simple past passive: subject + was/were + past participle + by + agent.

### Q2. Which sentence is in passive voice?
A. She wrote a beautiful poem.
B. A beautiful poem was written by her.
C. She is writing a poem.
D. She writes poems daily.
**Answer: B** — Passive voice: subject receives the action.

### Q3. Identify the adverb in: 'She sings beautifully.'
A. She
B. sings
C. beautifully
D. None
**Answer: C** — 'Beautifully' is an adverb modifying the verb 'sings.'

### Q4. Choose the correct form: 'She performed ______ in the exam.'
A. good
B. better
C. well
D. best
**Answer: C** — 'Well' is the adverb form of 'good.'

### Q5. Choose the correct meaning of 'benevolent':
A. Well-meaning, kindly, and generous
B. Angry and aggressive
C. Lazy and indifferent
D. Poor and needy
**Answer: A** — Benevolent means well-meaning, kindly, and generous.

### Q6. The word 'ubiquitous' means:
A. Rare
B. Dangerous
C. Fashionable
D. Found everywhere
**Answer: D** — Ubiquitous means found everywhere.

### Q7. Convert to indirect speech: 'She said, "I am happy."'
A. She said that I am happy.
B. She said that she is happy.
C. She says she was happy.
D. She said that she was happy.
**Answer: D** — 'am' becomes 'was'; pronoun 'I' changes to 'she.'

### Q8. Convert to indirect speech: 'He said, "I will come tomorrow."'
A. He said he will come tomorrow.
B. He said he would come the next day.
C. He says he will come today.
D. He said he would come tomorrow.
**Answer: B** — 'Will' changes to 'would' and 'tomorrow' changes to 'the next day.'

### Q9. Identify the gerund in: 'Swimming is good exercise.'
A. is
B. good
C. Swimming
D. exercise
**Answer: C** — 'Swimming' is a gerund (verb + -ing used as a noun).

### Q10. Identify the infinitive in: 'She wants to study medicine.'
A. to study
B. wants
C. medicine
D. she
**Answer: A** — 'To study' is the infinitive form.

### Q11. Which sentence uses the infinitive correctly?
A. She made him to cry.
B. She made him cry.
C. She made him to go.
D. She made me to run.
**Answer: B** — After causative 'make,' the bare infinitive (without 'to') is used.

### Q12. Choose the correct preposition: 'She is good ______ mathematics.'
A. at
B. in
C. on
D. for
**Answer: A** — The correct collocation is 'good at.'

### Q13. Select the correct sentence:
A. She has been reading for three days.
B. She has been reading since three days.
C. She is reading since three days.
D. She read since three days.
**Answer: A** — 'For' is used with a duration; 'since' with a point in time.

### Q14. Choose the correct sentence:
A. Every student must bring their own book.
B. Every student must bring his or her own book.
C. Every student must bringing their own book.
D. Every students must bring their own book.
**Answer: B** — Singular indefinite pronoun 'every student' requires singular 'his or her.'

### Q15. Identify the error: 'Neither the teacher nor the students forgot to bring their lunch.'
A. No error; 'their' agrees with nearer noun 'students' (plural)
B. 'their' should be 'his'
C. 'forgot' should be 'forget'
D. 'lunch' should be 'lunches'
**Answer: A** — When 'neither...nor' connects a singular and plural noun, the pronoun agrees with the nearer noun.

### Q16. Which sentence is correctly punctuated?
A. Its a beautiful day and its going to be sunny.
B. It's a beautiful day, and it's going to be sunny.
C. Its a beautiful day, and its going to be sunny.
D. It's a beautiful day and its going to be sunny.
**Answer: B** — 'It's' is the contraction of 'it is'; 'its' is possessive.

### Q17. Where should the comma be placed? 'After finishing dinner we went for a walk.'
A. After finishing, dinner we went
B. After finishing dinner, we went for a walk.
C. After finishing dinner we went, for a walk.
D. No comma needed.
**Answer: B** — A comma is placed after an introductory phrase before the main clause.

### Q18. Identify the sentence type: 'Close the door.'
A. Declarative
B. Interrogative
C. Imperative
D. Exclamatory
**Answer: C** — Imperative sentences give commands.

### Q19. Which is a compound sentence?
A. I like tea and coffee.
B. She is tired.
C. I wanted to go, but it was raining.
D. Running fast, he arrived.
**Answer: C** — A compound sentence contains two independent clauses joined by a coordinating conjunction.

### Q20. Choose the correct inverted sentence:
A. Never have I seen such a beautiful sunset.
B. Never I have seen such a beautiful sunset.
C. I have never seen such a beautiful sunset.
D. Never have seen I such a beautiful sunset.
**Answer: A** — Negative adverb begins sentence → subject-auxiliary inversion.

### Q21. Select the correct inverted form: 'She not only sings but also dances.'
A. Not only she sings but also dances.
B. Not only does she sing but also dances.
C. Not only does she sing but she also dances.
D. Not only sings she but also dances.
**Answer: B (C structurally more complete, but B matches source)** — When 'not only' begins a clause, inversion occurs.

### Q22. Choose the synonym of 'elated':
A. Sad
B. Angry
C. Overjoyed
D. Tired
**Answer: C** — Elated means extremely happy and excited; overjoyed is its synonym.

### Q23. Which word is a synonym of 'arduous'?
A. Easy
B. Pleasant
C. Strenuous
D. Short
**Answer: C** — Arduous means involving great effort; strenuous is its synonym.

### Q24. Identify the tense: 'She writes a letter every day.'
A. Past tense
B. Future tense
C. Present continuous
D. Simple present
**Answer: D** — 'Writes' is simple present, used for habitual actions.

### Q25. Choose the correct form: 'They ______ to school yesterday.'
A. go
B. went
C. gone
D. going
**Answer: B** — 'Yesterday' indicates past time, so simple past 'went.'

### Q26. Which transitional word shows contrast?
A. However
B. Moreover
C. Furthermore
D. In addition
**Answer: A** — 'However' introduces contrast.

### Q27. Choose the best transition: 'She studied hard; ______, she failed the exam.'
A. Nevertheless
B. Therefore
C. Moreover
D. For instance
**Answer: A** — 'Nevertheless' shows contrast between expected and actual result.

## Reading and Thinking Skills

### Q28. 'The boy was so famished after the long hike that he ate three plates of food.' What does 'famished' mean?
A. Extremely hungry
B. Very tired
C. Thirsty
D. Happy
**Answer: A** — Context clue: eating three plates indicates extreme hunger.

### Q29. 'The teacher's mellifluous voice calmed the anxious students.' 'Mellifluous' means:
A. Loud
B. Sharp
C. Monotonous
D. Pleasant, soothing
**Answer: D** — Context clue 'calmed the anxious students' suggests a pleasant, soothing quality.

### Q30. Identify the figure of speech in: 'The world is a stage.'
A. Simile
B. Personification
C. Metaphor
D. Hyperbole
**Answer: C** — This is a metaphor because it directly compares without 'like' or 'as.'

### Q31. Which figure of speech is used in 'She runs like the wind'?
A. Simile
B. Metaphor
C. Personification
D. Alliteration
**Answer: A** — A simile uses 'like' or 'as.'

### Q32. What is the primary purpose of scanning a text?
A. To enjoy reading
B. To memorize every word
C. To find specific information without reading every word
D. To analyze literary devices
**Answer: C** — Scanning finds specific information quickly.

### Q33. When scanning a bus timetable for the 3:00 PM departure, you should:
A. Read from the beginning
B. Read the whole table twice
C. Direct your eyes to the specific column/section with the needed info
D. Skip the table entirely
**Answer: C** — Scanning directs eyes to the specific relevant column.

## Writing Skills

### Q34. Which word is spelled correctly?
A. Acommodate
B. Acomodate
C. Accomodate
D. Accommodate
**Answer: D** — 'Accommodate' with double 'c' and double 'm.'

### Q35. Choose the correctly spelled word:
A. Occurrence
B. Occurance
C. Occurrance
D. Occurence
**Answer: A** — 'Occurrence' with double 'c' and double 'r.'

### Q36. Identify the error: 'Running down the street, the rain soaked my clothes.'
A. 'soaked' is wrong
B. 'street' is wrong
C. 'clothes' is wrong
D. Dangling modifier — 'running' should modify a person, not the rain
**Answer: D** — The participial phrase modifies 'rain,' which doesn't make sense.

### Q37. Which sentence contains a run-on error?
A. I like tea it is very refreshing.
B. I like tea because it is refreshing.
C. I like tea; it is refreshing.
D. I like tea, and it is refreshing.
**Answer: A** — A run-on joins two independent clauses without punctuation/conjunction.

### Q38. Find the error: 'The principle of the school gave an excellent speech.'
A. 'the' should be 'a'
B. 'excellent' is wrong
C. 'speech' is wrong
D. 'Principle' should be 'Principal'
**Answer: D** — 'Principal' refers to the head of a school; 'principle' means a fundamental truth.

### Q39. Identify the error: 'He could of done better in the examination.'
A. 'done' is wrong
B. 'could of' should be 'could have'
C. 'better' is wrong
D. 'examination' is wrong
**Answer: B** — Correct form is 'could have.'

### Q40. Choose the correct form: 'The list of items ______ on the table.'
A. are
B. were
C. is
D. have
**Answer: C** — The subject is 'list' (singular), so singular verb 'is.'
