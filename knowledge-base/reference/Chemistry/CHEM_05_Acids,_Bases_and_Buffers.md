⭐ **Q125.** A buffer solution contains equal concentrations of X⁻ and HX. The Kb for X⁻ is 10⁻¹⁰. The pH of the buffer is:

**Q126.** [ 2012] When pH = pKa, the concentration of salt is:
- A. Equal to one
- B. Less than acid concentration
- C. At zero
- D. Equal to that of the weak acid
**Answer: D**

---

**Q127.** [ 2012] What is the pH of a buffer containing 0.1M weak acid (pKa = 5) and 0.1M salt?
- A. 1
- B. 3
- C. 5
- D. 7
**Answer: C**
