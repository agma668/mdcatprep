⭐ **Q83.** How many sperms and ova will be produced from 100 primary spermatocytes and 100 primary oocytes?
- A. 100 sperms and 100 ova
- B. 100 sperms and 400 ova
- C. 400 sperms and 100 ova
**Answer: C** [Official Sample]

**Q85.** [ 2021] The similarity between an ovum and a sperm is:
- A. They both are haploid
- B. Both are Diploid
- C. Both have the same size
- D. Both have the same genetic information
**Answer: A**

---

**Q86.** [ 2021] If the Graafian follicle is degenerated without forming corpus luteum:
- A. Ovulation does not occur
- B. Pregnancy is established
- C. The menstrual cycle is not started
- D. None of the above
**Answer: D**
