**Q245.** Hemophilia — parents have hemophilic son, normal son, and hemophilic daughter. Genotype of parents?
- A. XᴴXᴴ × XʰY
- B. XᴴXʰ × XᴴY
- C. XᴴXʰ × XʰY

**Q275.** [ 2013] Pfund series lies in:
- A. UV region
- B. Visible region
- C. IR region

**Answer: C** (Pfund = infrared, n≥6 to n=5)

**Q276.** [ 2013] Angular momentum has same unit as:
- A. Gravitational constant
- B. Eddies constant
- C. Planck's constant
- D. Stress
**Answer: C** (Both J·s)

---

**Q277.** [ 2013] Hollow brass sphere positively charged — electric potential INSIDE:
- A. Zero
- B. Less than potential on surface
- C. Greater than potential on surface
- D. Equal to potential on surface
**Answer: D** (Uniform potential inside a conducting sphere)

---

**Q278.** [ 2013] A charge of 0.1C accelerated through PD of 1000V — KE acquired:
- A. 189 J
- B. 100 J
- C. 289 J
- D. 300 J
**Answer: B** (KE = qV = 0.1 × 1000 = 100 J)

---

**Q279.** [ 2013] If tension in string made 4 times, speed of sound in string:
- A. V
- B. 2V
- C. 4V
- D. None
**Answer: B** (v ∝ √T, so if T→4T, v→2v)

---

**Q280.** [ 2013] Penetrating power of X-ray increased by increasing:
- A. Frequency
- B. Velocity of electron
- C. Intensity
- D. None
**Answer: B** (Higher electron velocity = higher energy X-rays)

---

**Q281.** [ 2013] Cross product of a vector with itself:
- A. 1
- B. Zero
- C. Negative
- D. None
**Answer: B** (A × A = |A|²sin0° = 0)

---

**Q282.** [ 2013] At height equal to radius of earth, value of g:
- A. g
- B. g/2
- C. g/4
- D. Zero
**Answer: C** (g' = g/(1+h/R)² = g/4 when h=R)
