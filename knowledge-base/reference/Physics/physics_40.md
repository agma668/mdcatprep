## Alternating Current

### Q1. In a purely resistive AC circuit, current and voltage are:
A. In phase
B. 90° out of phase
C. 180° out of phase
D. 45° out of phase
**Answer: A** — In a purely resistive circuit, no reactance exists, so current and voltage are in phase.

### Q2. The speed of electromagnetic waves in vacuum is:
A. 3 × 10⁶ m/s
B. 3 × 10⁸ m/s
C. 3 × 10¹⁰ m/s
D. 3 × 10⁵ m/s
**Answer: B** — All EM waves travel at c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s in vacuum.

### Q3. The RMS value of AC voltage with peak value V₀ is:
A. V₀
B. 2V₀
C. V₀/2
D. V₀/√2
**Answer: D** — Vrms = V₀/√2 = 0.707 V₀.

## Atomic Spectra

### Q4. Hydrogen line spectrum is produced when electrons transition from:
A. Lower to higher energy levels
B. Nucleus to orbit
C. One atom to another
D. Higher (n₂) to lower (n₁) energy levels
**Answer: D** — Electrons fall from higher to lower energy levels, emitting photons of specific wavelengths.

## Current Electricity

### Q5. A battery of EMF 12 V and internal resistance 2Ω is connected to 4Ω external resistance. Terminal voltage is:
A. 12 V
B. 8 V
C. 6 V
D. 4 V
**Answer: B** — I = EMF/(R+r) = 12/6 = 2A. V_terminal = EMF - Ir = 12 - 4 = 8V.

### Q6. A 60W bulb is connected to 240V supply. Current through it is:
A. 0.25 A
B. 0.5 A
C. 1 A
D. 4 A
**Answer: A** — P = VI → I = P/V = 60/240 = 0.25A.

### Q7. A resistor has 12V across it and 3A through it. Its resistance is:
A. 36 Ω
B. 9 Ω
C. 4 Ω
D. 15 Ω
**Answer: C** — R = V/I = 12/3 = 4Ω.

### Q8. A wire of length 2m, cross-section 1mm², resistivity 1.7×10⁻⁸ Ω·m. Its resistance is:
A. 0.34 Ω
B. 3.4 Ω
C. 34 Ω
D. 0.034 Ω
**Answer: D** — R = ρL/A = 1.7×10⁻⁸ × 2 / 1×10⁻⁶ = 0.034Ω.

### Q9. Electric current is defined as the rate of flow of:
A. Energy
B. Voltage
C. Charge
D. Electrons only
**Answer: C** — I = ΔQ/Δt; current = charge passing per unit time.

## Dawn of Modern Physics

### Q10. Planck's constant h has SI units of:
A. J/m
B. J·m
C. J·s
D. kg·m/s
**Answer: C** — Planck's constant h = 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s.

## Electromagnetic Induction

### Q11. Faraday's law states that induced EMF equals:
A. Magnetic flux
B. Negative rate of change of magnetic flux
C. Current × resistance
D. Voltage / time
**Answer: B** — Faraday's law: EMF = −dΦ/dt.

### Q12. Lenz's law states that the induced current flows to:
A. Increase the flux
B. Create maximum flux
C. Be in the same direction as the field
D. Oppose the change in flux that produced it
**Answer: D** — Lenz's law: induced current opposes the cause producing it, consistent with energy conservation.

### Q13. A step-up transformer has 100 primary and 500 secondary turns. Input 220V. Output voltage is:
A. 44V
B. 110V
C. 550V
D. 1100V
**Answer: D** — Vs/Vp = Ns/Np → Vs = 220 × (500/100) = 1100V.

### Q14. A transformer operates on AC and not DC because it requires:
A. High voltage
B. Low current
C. Changing magnetic field
D. Steady magnetic field
**Answer: D (corrected: it requires CHANGING flux)** — A steady current gives constant flux inducing nothing. Alternating current continually changes the flux.

## Electromagnetism

### Q15. The magnetic force on a charge moving parallel to the magnetic field is:
A. Maximum
B. Proportional to velocity
C. Inversely proportional to mass
D. Zero
**Answer: D** — Force depends on sinθ; when parallel, sin0° = 0 and no force acts.

### Q16. Magnetic flux through area A in field B at angle θ to the normal is:
A. BA sinθ
B. BA/tanθ
C. B/A
D. BA cosθ
**Answer: D** — Φ = BA cosθ.

### Q17. The SI unit of magnetic flux density (B) is:
A. Weber
B. Henry
C. Tesla
D. Gauss
**Answer: C** — Magnetic flux density B is measured in Tesla (T).

### Q18. A proton moves at 3×10⁶ m/s perpendicular to B=0.5T. Radius of path is approximately:
A. 0.626 m
B. 6.26 m
C. 0.006 m
D. 0.063 m
**Answer: D** — r = mv/(qB) ≈ 0.063m.

## Electronics

### Q19. A PN junction diode conducts easily when:
A. Reverse biased
B. Not connected
C. In equilibrium
D. Forward biased
**Answer: D** — Forward bias reduces the potential barrier, allowing current to flow.

### Q20. The chief disadvantage of a half wave rectifier compared with a full wave circuit is its:
A. Higher cost
B. Lower mean DC output and larger ripple
C. More components
D. Higher output voltage
**Answer: B** — Half of every cycle is discarded; lower mean DC voltage and larger ripple.

### Q21. A half-wave rectifier uses:
A. A single diode
B. Two diodes
C. Four diodes
D. A transistor
**Answer: A** — Half-wave rectifier: a single diode passes only one half of the AC cycle.

### Q22. Which quantity is unaffected by increasing the doping level of a pn junction?
A. Barrier height
B. Electronic charge
C. Breakdown voltage
D. Depletion width
**Answer: B** — Electronic charge is a universal constant unaffected by doping.

## Electrostatics

### Q23. The capacitance of a parallel plate capacitor increases if:
A. Plate area decreases
B. Distance increases
C. Dielectric is removed
D. A dielectric is inserted between the plates
**Answer: D** — C = ε₀εᵣA/d. Inserting a dielectric (εᵣ > 1) increases capacitance.

### Q24. Coulomb's constant k in SI units is approximately:
A. 9 × 10⁶ N·m²/C²
B. 9 × 10¹² N·m²/C²
C. 9 × 10⁹ N·m²/C²
D. 9 × 10³ N·m²/C²
**Answer: C** — k = 1/(4πε₀) = 9 × 10⁹ N·m²·C⁻².

### Q25. The electric field at distance r from a point charge Q is:
A. kQr
B. kQ/r³
C. kQr²
D. kQ/r²
**Answer: D** — E = kQ/r².

### Q26. The electric field due to an infinite plane sheet of charge is:
A. σ/2ε₀ (uniform, independent of distance)
B. σ/r²
C. σ/ε₀r
D. Depends on distance
**Answer: A** — By Gauss's law, E = σ/2ε₀ on each side of an infinite sheet.

### Q27. Electric field lines originate from:
A. Negative charges
B. Positive charges
C. Neutral points
D. Magnetic poles
**Answer: B** — Electric field lines emerge from positive charges and terminate on negative charges.

### Q28. The electric potential at distance r from point charge Q is:
A. kQr
B. kQ/r²
C. kQ/r
D. kQr²
**Answer: C** — V = kQ/r.

## Fluid Dynamics

### Q29. Bernoulli's equation is based on conservation of:
A. Mass
B. Momentum
C. Energy
D. Charge
**Answer: C** — Bernoulli's equation is a statement of conservation of energy for flowing fluids.

### Q30. The equation of continuity states that for an incompressible fluid:
A. A₁v₁ = A₂v₂
B. P₁ + P₂ = constant
C. F = ma
D. V = IR
**Answer: A** — Equation of continuity: volume flow rate is constant.

### Q31. According to Stokes' law, drag force on a sphere is:
A. F = 6πηrv
B. F = 3πηrv
C. F = πηrv
D. F = 6πηr²v
**Answer: A** — Stokes' law: F = 6πηrv.

### Q32. Flow is turbulent when Reynolds number is:
A. Less than 1000
B. Exactly 2000
C. Less than 500
D. Greater than 2000
**Answer: D** — Flow becomes turbulent when Re > 2000.

### Q33. At terminal velocity, the net force on a falling body is:
A. Zero
B. Equal to weight
C. Maximum
D. Equal to drag only
**Answer: A** — At terminal velocity, weight = drag force, so net force is zero.

## Force and Motion

### Q34. A car accelerates from 0 to 20 m/s in 4s. The acceleration is:
A. 10 m/s²
B. 2.5 m/s²
C. 80 m/s²
D. 5 m/s²
**Answer: D** — a = (20-0)/4 = 5 m/s².

### Q35. In a perfectly inelastic collision, which quantity is conserved?
A. Momentum only
B. Kinetic energy only
C. Both momentum and kinetic energy
D. Neither
**Answer: A** — In a perfectly inelastic collision, momentum is conserved but kinetic energy is not.

### Q36. A person walks 3 km east and then 4 km north. The displacement is:
A. 7 km
B. 1 km
C. 5 km
D. 12 km
**Answer: C** — Displacement = √(3² + 4²) = √25 = 5 km.

### Q37. The slope of a displacement-time graph gives:
A. Velocity
B. Acceleration
C. Force
D. Momentum
**Answer: A** — The slope of s-t graph = ds/dt = velocity.

### Q38. A 2kg ball at 5m/s collides and sticks to a stationary 3kg ball. Their common velocity is:
A. 1 m/s
B. 3 m/s
C. 2 m/s
D. 2.5 m/s
**Answer: C** — Conservation of momentum: 2×5 = 5v → v = 2 m/s.

### Q39. Newton's first law of motion is also called the law of:
A. Action and reaction
B. Conservation of momentum
C. Gravitation
D. Inertia
**Answer: D** — Newton's first law: a body maintains its state unless acted upon by a force — law of inertia.

### Q40. A force of 10N acts on a 5kg object. Its acceleration is:
A. 50 m/s²
B. 0.5 m/s²
C. 2 m/s²
D. 10 m/s²
**Answer: C** — a = F/m = 10/5 = 2 m/s².
