## CHEM-01 — Atomic Structure & Quantum Numbers

---

⭐ **Q98.** When two electrons move in the same orbital, they do not have the same values of four quantum numbers. This is known as:
- A. The Aufbau principle
- B. Pauli's Exclusion principle
- C. Hund's Rule
**Answer: B** [Official Sample]

---

⭐ **Q99.** The hybridization of an oxygen atom in water is:
- A. sp
- B. sp²
- C. sp³
**Answer: C** [Official Sample]

---

**Q100.** [ 2012] In a discharge tube, cathode ray ejects electron from internal shell of metal atom. Which rays are emitted when electron falls from external shells to fill gap?
- A. Beta Rays
- B. Gamma Rays
- C. Alpha Rays
- D. X Rays
**Answer: D**
