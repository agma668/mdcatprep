# PMDC MDCAT-2025 official syllabus map

Source: `_research/syllabus/PMDC_Uniform_Curriculum_MDCAT-2025_Final_26-05-2025.pdf`

180 MCQs | 3 hours | paper-based | **no negative marking** 
Difficulty: 15% easy / 70% moderate / 15% difficult 
Pass marks: 55% (MBBS), 50% (BDS) 
English and Logical Reasoning are deliberately excluded from this map.

### Known defects in the source PDF (and how they are handled here)

- Chemistry unit **4 (Liquids)** is missing from the source PDF's Unit column — PMDC's own table jumps 3 -> 5, and the liquid-state topics (vapour pressure, boiling point, hydrogen bonding) are folded into unit 5's row. Treat Liquids as an examinable unit regardless.
- Biology units **15 (Homeostasis)** and **16 (Biotechnology)** share one table row in the source; Biotechnology is listed below as its own unit.
- Unit names for Chemistry 16/17 spill into the Topics column in the source PDF ('...AND' + 'PHENOLS' / 'KETONES'); both are restored to their full names.
