# Syllabus Reactions - Part 1 (Hydrocarbons & Benzene)

## 16.3 Alkanes
**16.3.3 Free Radical Substitution (Halogenation)**
- Methane: <span class="reaction-eq">CH4 + Cl2 ⟶ CH3Cl + HCl</span> (in presence of UV light)
 - Continues to: <span class="reaction-eq">CH2Cl2</span>, <span class="reaction-eq">CHCl3</span> (Chloroform), <span class="reaction-eq">CCl4</span> (Carbon tetrachloride)
- Ethane: <span class="reaction-eq">CH3-CH3 + Cl2 ⟶ CH3-CH2-Cl + HCl</span>

## 16.4 Alkenes (Ethene)
**16.4.1 Preparation of Ethene**
- Dehydration of alcohol: <span class="reaction-eq">CH3-CH2-OH ⟶ CH2=CH2 + H2O</span> (conc. H2SO4, 180°C or Al2O3, 350°C)
- Dehydrohalogenation of alkyl halide: <span class="reaction-eq">CH3-CH2-Cl + KOH(alc) ⟶ CH2=CH2 + KCl + H2O</span>

**16.4.2 Reactions of Ethene**
- Hydrogenation: <span class="reaction-eq">CH2=CH2 + H2 ⟶ CH3-CH3</span> (Ni, 250°C)
- Hydration: <span class="reaction-eq">CH2=CH2 + H2O ⟶ CH3-CH2-OH</span> (H2SO4 catalyst)
- Hydrohalogenation: <span class="reaction-eq">CH2=CH2 + HX ⟶ CH3-CH2-X</span> (e.g., HCl, HBr)
- Halogenation: <span class="reaction-eq">CH2=CH2 + Br2 ⟶ CH2(Br)-CH2(Br)</span> (in CCl4, Orange-red decolorizes)
- Halohydration (Addition of HOX): <span class="reaction-eq">CH2=CH2 + X2 + H2O ⟶ CH2(OH)-CH2(X) + HX</span> (Formation of halohydrin)
- Epoxidation: <span class="reaction-eq">CH2=CH2 + 1/2 O2 ⟶ Ethylene Oxide (C2H4O)</span> (Ag catalyst, 300°C) OR reaction with peroxyacids.
- Ozonolysis: <span class="reaction-eq">CH2=CH2 + O3 ⟶ [Ozonide] ⟶ 2HCHO + H2O2</span> (Zn/H2O)
- Polymerisation: <span class="reaction-eq">n(CH2=CH2) ⟶ [-CH2-CH2-]_n</span> (High T/P, trace O2 or Ziegler-Natta)

## 16.5 Alkynes
**16.5.2 Preparation of Alkynes**
- Elimination (Dehydrohalogenation of vicinal dihalides): <span class="reaction-eq">CH2(Br)-CH2(Br) + 2KOH(alc) ⟶ HC≡CH + 2KBr + 2H2O</span>

**16.5.3 Acidic nature (Reaction with Metals)**
- With Sodium: <span class="reaction-eq">HC≡CH + 2Na ⟶ Na-C≡C-Na + H2</span> (Sodium acetylide)
- With Ammoniacal AgNO3: <span class="reaction-eq">HC≡CH + 2AgNO3 + 2NH4OH ⟶ Ag-C≡C-Ag + 2NH4NO3 + 2H2O</span> (White ppt)
- With Ammoniacal Cu2Cl2: <span class="reaction-eq">HC≡CH + Cu2Cl2 + 2NH4OH ⟶ Cu-C≡C-Cu + 2NH4Cl + 2H2O</span> (Red ppt)

**16.5.4 Chemistry of Alkynes**
- Hydrogenation: <span class="reaction-eq">HC≡CH + 2H2 ⟶ CH3-CH3</span> (Ni) or to Ethene using Lindlar's/Birch.
- Hydrohalogenation: <span class="reaction-eq">HC≡CH + 2HX ⟶ CH3-CHX2</span> (Geminal dihalide, follows Markovnikov's)
- Hydration: <span class="reaction-eq">HC≡CH + H2O ⟶ [CH2=CH-OH] ⟶ CH3-CHO</span> (HgSO4/H2SO4, 80°C)
- Bromination: <span class="reaction-eq">HC≡CH + 2Br2 ⟶ CHBr2-CHBr2</span> (1,1,2,2-Tetrabromoethane)
- Ozonolysis: <span class="reaction-eq">HC≡CH + O3 ⟶ [Ozonide] ⟶ HCOOH + HCOOH</span> (Cleavage to Formic/Carboxylic Acids)

**16.5.5 Combustion**
- Alkanes: <span class="reaction-eq">CH4 + 2O2 ⟶ CO2 + 2H2O + Heat</span>
- Alkenes: <span class="reaction-eq">C2H4 + 3O2 ⟶ 2CO2 + 2H2O + Heat</span>
- Alkynes: <span class="reaction-eq">2C2H2 + 5O2 ⟶ 4CO2 + 2H2O + Heat</span> (Oxy-acetylene flame)

## 16.6 Benzene and Substituted Benzene
**16.6.4 Chemistry of Benzene (and Toluene)**
- Nitration: <span class="reaction-eq">C6H6 + HNO3 ⟶ C6H5-NO2 + H2O</span> (conc. H2SO4, 50°C)
- Sulphonation: <span class="reaction-eq">C6H6 + H2SO4(fuming) ⟶ C6H5-SO3H + H2O</span> (Heat)
- Halogenation: <span class="reaction-eq">C6H6 + Cl2 ⟶ C6H5-Cl + HCl</span> (FeCl3 or AlCl3 catalyst)
- Friedel-Crafts Alkylation: <span class="reaction-eq">C6H6 + CH3Cl ⟶ C6H5-CH3 + HCl</span> (AlCl3)
- Friedel-Crafts Acylation: <span class="reaction-eq">C6H6 + CH3COCl ⟶ C6H5-CO-CH3 + HCl</span> (AlCl3)
- For Methylbenzene (Toluene), these same EAS reactions will yield ortho/para substituted products (e.g., Nitration of Toluene yields o-nitrotoluene and p-nitrotoluene, ultimately TNT).

# Syllabus Reactions - Part 2 (Alkyl Halides & Amines)

## 17.1 Alkyl Halides
**17.1.4 Preparation of Alkyl Halides (from Alcohol)**
- With HX: <span class="reaction-eq">R-OH + HX ⟶ R-X + H2O</span> (ZnCl2 catalyst for HCl/Lucas Reagent)
- With SOCl2: <span class="reaction-eq">R-OH + SOCl2 ⟶ R-Cl + SO2 + HCl</span> (Thionyl chloride / Darzen's Process)
- With PCl3: <span class="reaction-eq">3R-OH + PCl3 ⟶ 3R-Cl + H3PO3</span>
- With PCl5: <span class="reaction-eq">R-OH + PCl5 ⟶ R-Cl + POCl3 + HCl</span>

## 17.2 Nucleophilic Substitution Reactions
**17.2.1 Mechanism of SN1 and SN2**
- SN2 (Bimolecular, 1-step, 1° halides): <span class="reaction-eq">Nu⁻ + R-X ⟶ Nu-R + X⁻</span> (Inversion of configuration)
- SN1 (Unimolecular, 2-step, 3° halides):
 - Step 1: <span class="reaction-eq">R-X ⟶ R⁺ + X⁻</span> (Slow, carbocation formation)
 - Step 2: <span class="reaction-eq">R⁺ + Nu⁻ ⟶ R-Nu</span> (Fast, racemate mixture)

## 17.3 Elimination Reaction
**17.3.1 Mechanism of E1 and E2**
- E2 (Bimolecular, 1-step, strong base, 1°/2° halides): Base removes β-proton simultaneously as leaving group leaves, forming alkene.
- E1 (Unimolecular, 2-step, weak base, 3° halides): Forms carbocation first, then base removes β-proton forming alkene.

## 17.4 Organo-Metallic Compounds (Grignard Reagent)
**17.4.1 Preparation of Grignard Reagent**
- <span class="reaction-eq">R-X + Mg ⟶ R-Mg-X</span> (in dry ether)

**17.4.2 Chemical reaction of Grignard Reagent**
- With Aldehydes (Formaldehyde): <span class="reaction-eq">R-Mg-X + HCHO ⟶ [Adduct] ⟶ R-CH2-OH</span> (1° Alcohol)
- With Aldehydes (Acetaldehyde): <span class="reaction-eq">R-Mg-X + CH3CHO ⟶ [Adduct] ⟶ R-CH(OH)-CH3</span> (2° Alcohol)
- With Ketones (Acetone): <span class="reaction-eq">R-Mg-X + CH3COCH3 ⟶ [Adduct] ⟶ R-C(CH3)2-OH</span> (3° Alcohol)
- With Esters (e.g., Ethyl formate): Forms Aldehyde first, which reacts with another mole of RMgX to form 2° Alcohol. Other esters form Ketones then 3° Alcohols.
- With Carbon Dioxide: <span class="reaction-eq">R-Mg-X + CO2 ⟶ R-CO-OMgX ⟶ R-COOH</span> (Carboxylic Acid)

## 17.5 Amines
**17.5.5 Preparation of Amines**
- a. Alkylation of NH3 (Hofmann's method): <span class="reaction-eq">NH3 + R-X ⟶ R-NH2 + HX</span> (Continues to 2°, 3°, and quaternary salts)
- b. Reduction of Nitriles: <span class="reaction-eq">R-CN + 4[H] ⟶ R-CH2-NH2</span> (LiAlH4 or H2/Ni)
- c. Reduction of Nitro compounds: <span class="reaction-eq">R-NO2 + 6[H] ⟶ R-NH2 + 2H2O</span> (Sn/HCl or Fe/HCl)
- d. Reduction of Amides: <span class="reaction-eq">R-CO-NH2 + 4[H] ⟶ R-CH2-NH2 + H2O</span> (LiAlH4)

**17.5.6 Chemical reaction of Amines**
- a. Alkylation with RX: <span class="reaction-eq">R-NH2 + R'-X ⟶ R-NH-R' + HX</span> (Secondary amine formation)
- b. Reaction with aldehydes and ketones: <span class="reaction-eq">R-NH2 + >C=O ⟶ >C=N-R + H2O</span> (Imine / Schiff base formation)

**17.5.7 Preparation of Amides and Diazonium Salts**
- Amides (from Acid Chloride/Anhydride + Amine): <span class="reaction-eq">R-COCl + R'-NH2 ⟶ R-CO-NH-R' + HCl</span>
- Diazonium Salts (from 1° aromatic amine): <span class="reaction-eq">C6H5-NH2 + NaNO2 + 2HCl ⟶ C6H5-N2⁺Cl⁻ + NaCl + 2H2O</span> (0-5°C, Diazotization)

# Syllabus Reactions - Part 3 (Alcohols, Phenols & Ethers)

## 18.1 Alcohols
**18.1.3 Lucas Test (Distinguish 1°, 2°, 3° alcohols)**
- <span class="reaction-eq">R-OH + HCl ⟶ R-Cl(turbidity) + H2O</span> (ZnCl2)
 - 3° Alcohol: Immediate turbidity
 - 2° Alcohol: Turbidity after 5 minutes
 - 1° Alcohol: No reaction at room temperature

**18.1.4 Iodoform Test (Methanol vs Ethanol)**
- Ethanol: <span class="reaction-eq">CH3CH2OH + 4I2 + 6NaOH ⟶ CHI3(yellow ppt) + HCOONa + 5NaI + 5H2O</span>
- Methanol: No reaction (no yellow ppt).

**18.1.5 Preparation of Alcohols (by Reduction)**
- From Aldehydes: <span class="reaction-eq">R-CHO + 2[H] ⟶ R-CH2-OH</span> (1° Alcohol; NaBH4, LiAlH4, or H2/Ni)
- From Ketones: <span class="reaction-eq">R-CO-R' + 2[H] ⟶ R-CH(OH)-R'</span> (2° Alcohol; NaBH4, LiAlH4)
- From Carboxylic Acids: <span class="reaction-eq">R-COOH + 4[H] ⟶ R-CH2-OH + H2O</span> (LiAlH4)
- From Esters: <span class="reaction-eq">R-COOR' + 4[H] ⟶ R-CH2-OH + R'-OH</span> (LiAlH4)

**18.1.7 Reactions of Alcohols**
- a. Preparation of Ethers: <span class="reaction-eq">2R-OH ⟶ R-O-R + H2O</span> (H2SO4, 140°C)
- b. Preparation of Esters (Esterification): <span class="reaction-eq">R-OH + R'-COOH ⇌ R'-COOR + H2O</span> (H+ catalyzed)
- c. Oxidative Cleavage of 1,2-diols: <span class="reaction-eq">R-CH(OH)-CH(OH)-R' + HIO4 ⟶ R-CHO + R'-CHO + HIO3 + H2O</span> (Periodic acid)

## 18.2 Phenols
**18.2.3 Preparation of Phenols**
- From Benzene Sulphonic Acid: <span class="reaction-eq">C6H5SO3H + 3NaOH ⟶ C6H5ONa + Na2SO3 + 2H2O</span> (320°C). Then: <span class="reaction-eq">C6H5ONa + HCl ⟶ C6H5OH + NaCl</span>
- From Chlorobenzene (Dow's Process): <span class="reaction-eq">C6H5Cl + 2NaOH ⟶ C6H5ONa + NaCl + H2O</span> (360°C, 150 atm). Then: <span class="reaction-eq">C6H5ONa + HCl ⟶ C6H5OH + NaCl</span>
- From Cumene (Acidic Oxidation): <span class="reaction-eq">C6H5-CH(CH3)2 + O2 ⟶ [Cumene Hydroperoxide] ⟶ C6H5OH + CH3COCH3</span> (H2SO4, H2O)
- From Diazonium Salts (Hydrolysis): <span class="reaction-eq">C6H5-N2⁺Cl⁻ + H2O ⟶ C6H5OH + N2 + HCl</span> (Warm / Boil)

**18.2.4 Reactivity of Phenol**
- Reaction with Na metal: <span class="reaction-eq">2C6H5OH + 2Na ⟶ 2C6H5ONa + H2</span> (Sodium phenoxide)
- Oxidation: <span class="reaction-eq">C6H5OH + [O] ⟶ p-Benzoquinone + H2O</span> (Na2Cr2O7 / H2SO4)
- Electrophilic Aromatic Substitution (EAS): -OH is a strong activating ortho/para director. Example: <span class="reaction-eq">C6H5OH + 3Br2(aq) ⟶ 2,4,6-Tribromophenol (white ppt) + 3HBr</span>

## 18.3 Ethers
**18.3.3 Preparation of Ethers**
- a. Williamson Synthesis: <span class="reaction-eq">R-ONa + R'-X ⟶ R-O-R' + NaX</span> (Best for asymmetrical ethers)
- b. Alkyl halides with dry silver oxide: <span class="reaction-eq">2R-X + Ag2O(dry) ⟶ R-O-R + 2AgX</span>
- c. Alcohols with excess H2SO4: <span class="reaction-eq">2R-OH ⟶ R-O-R + H2O</span> (H2SO4, 140°C)

# Syllabus Reactions - Part 4 (Aldehydes and Ketones)

## 19.3 Preparation of Aldehydes and Ketones
**19.3.1 Preparation by:**
- a. Ozonolysis of alkene: <span class="reaction-eq">R-CH=CH-R' + O3 ⟶ [Ozonide] ⟶ R-CHO + R'-CHO + H2O2</span> (Zn/H2O)
- b. Hydration of alkyne: <span class="reaction-eq">HC≡CH + H2O ⟶ CH3CHO</span> (Acetaldehyde; HgSO4/H2SO4, 80°C)
- c. Oxidation of alcohol:
 - 1° Alcohol to Aldehyde: <span class="reaction-eq">R-CH2OH + [O] ⟶ R-CHO + H2O</span> (K2Cr2O7/H2SO4)
 - 2° Alcohol to Ketone: <span class="reaction-eq">R-CH(OH)-R' + [O] ⟶ R-CO-R' + H2O</span>
- d. Friedel-Crafts acylation of aromatic compound: <span class="reaction-eq">C6H6 + CH3COCl ⟶ C6H5-CO-CH3 + HCl</span> (AlCl3)

## 19.4 Reaction of Aldehydes and Ketones
**19.4.1 Base catalysed nucleophilic addition**
- a. Addition of HCN: <span class="reaction-eq">>C=O + HCN ⟶ >C(OH)(CN)</span> (Cyanohydrin formation)
- b. Addition of Grignard reagent: <span class="reaction-eq">>C=O + R-Mg-X ⟶ >C(R)(OMgX) ⟶ >C(R)(OH)</span> (Yields Alcohols)
- c. Addition of sodium bisulphite: <span class="reaction-eq">>C=O + NaHSO3 ⟶ >C(OH)(SO3Na)</span> (White ppt, purification)
- d. Aldol condensation: <span class="reaction-eq">2CH3CHO ⟶ CH3-CH(OH)-CH2-CHO</span> (dil. NaOH, requires alpha-hydrogen)
- e. Cannizzaro's reaction: <span class="reaction-eq">2HCHO + NaOH ⟶ CH3OH + HCOONa</span> (Disproportionation, no alpha-hydrogen)
- f. Haloform (iodoform) reaction: <span class="reaction-eq">CH3-CO-R + 3I2 + 4NaOH ⟶ CHI3(yellow) + R-COONa + 3NaI + 3H2O</span>

**19.4.2 Acid catalysed nucleophilic addition**
- a. Polymerisation:
 - Formaldehyde: <span class="reaction-eq">n(HCHO) ⟶ Metaformaldehyde</span> (or Trioxane)
 - Acetaldehyde: <span class="reaction-eq">n(CH3CHO) ⟶ Paraldehyde</span> (H2SO4)
- b. Addition of ammonia derivatives (e.g., 2,4-DNPH, Hydroxylamine): <span class="reaction-eq">>C=O + H2N-G ⟶ >C=N-G + H2O</span> (Condensation)
- c. Addition of alcohols (Acetal formation): <span class="reaction-eq">R-CHO + 2R'OH ⟶ R-CH(OR')2 + H2O</span> (dry HCl gas)

**19.4.3 Reduction of Aldehydes and Ketones**
- a. Clemmensen reduction: <span class="reaction-eq">>C=O + 4[H] ⟶ >CH2 + H2O</span> (Zn-Hg / conc. HCl; Carbonyl to Alkane)
- b. Wolff-Kishner reduction: <span class="reaction-eq">>C=O + 4[H] ⟶ >CH2 + H2O</span> (NH2NH2 / KOH, heat; Carbonyl to Alkane)
- c. Hydride reagents: <span class="reaction-eq">>C=O + 2[H] ⟶ >CH-OH</span> (NaBH4 or LiAlH4; Carbonyl to Alcohol)

**19.4.4 Oxidation reactions**
- Aldehydes: <span class="reaction-eq">R-CHO + [O] ⟶ R-COOH</span> (Easily oxidized by K2Cr2O7/H2SO4, Tollen's, or Fehling's)
- Ketones: Resist mild oxidation. Strong oxidation (conc. HNO3) cleaves C-C bonds forming mixture of carboxylic acids.

# Syllabus Reactions - Part 5 (Carboxylic Acids & Derivatives)

## 20.4 Preparation of Carboxylic Acid
**20.4.1 Preparation by:**
- a. Grignard reagent: <span class="reaction-eq">R-Mg-X + CO2 ⟶ R-CO-OMgX ⟶ R-COOH</span> (followed by acid hydrolysis)
- b. Hydrolysis of nitriles: <span class="reaction-eq">R-C≡N + 2H2O + HCl ⟶ R-COOH + NH4Cl</span> (Acid catalyzed)
- c. Oxidation of primary alcohol: <span class="reaction-eq">R-CH2OH + 2[O] ⟶ R-COOH + H2O</span> (K2Cr2O7 / H2SO4)
- d. Oxidation of aldehydes: <span class="reaction-eq">R-CHO + [O] ⟶ R-COOH</span>
- e. Oxidation of alkyl benzene: <span class="reaction-eq">C6H5-CH3 + 3[O] ⟶ C6H5-COOH + H2O</span> (KMnO4 / H+, Benzoic acid prep)

## 20.5 & 20.6 Reactivity and Reactions
**20.5.1 Reactions of Carboxylic Acid involving:**
- a. Hydrogen atom of carboxyl group (Acidic nature): <span class="reaction-eq">2R-COOH + 2Na ⟶ 2R-COONa + H2</span> or <span class="reaction-eq">R-COOH + NaOH ⟶ R-COONa + H2O</span>
- b. Hydroxyl group of carboxyl group: <span class="reaction-eq">R-COOH + PCl5 ⟶ R-COCl + POCl3 + HCl</span>
- c. Carboxyl group as a whole (Reduction): <span class="reaction-eq">R-COOH + 4[H] ⟶ R-CH2OH + H2O</span> (LiAlH4)

**20.5.2 Reactivity Order of Derivatives**
- Acyl Halides > Acid Anhydrides > Esters > Amides

**20.6.1 Preparation of Derivatives**
- Acyl halides: <span class="reaction-eq">R-COOH + SOCl2 ⟶ R-COCl + SO2 + HCl</span>
- Acid anhydrides: <span class="reaction-eq">2R-COOH ⟶ (R-CO)2O + H2O</span> (P2O5, heat, dehydration)
- Esters (Esterification): <span class="reaction-eq">R-COOH + R'-OH ⇌ R-COOR' + H2O</span> (conc. H2SO4)
- Amides: <span class="reaction-eq">R-COOH + NH3 ⟶ R-COONH4 ⟶ R-CONH2 + H2O</span> (Heat)

**20.6.3 Reactions of Carboxylic Acid Derivatives**
- a. Friedel-Crafts acylation using acyl halide: <span class="reaction-eq">C6H6 + R-COCl ⟶ C6H5-CO-R + HCl</span> (AlCl3)
- b. Hydrolysis (yields Carboxylic Acid):
 - Acid anhydrides: <span class="reaction-eq">(R-CO)2O + H2O ⟶ 2R-COOH</span>
 - Esters: <span class="reaction-eq">R-COOR' + H2O ⟶ R-COOH + R'-OH</span> (Acid or base catalyzed)
 - Amides: <span class="reaction-eq">R-CONH2 + H2O + HCl ⟶ R-COOH + NH4Cl</span>
- c. Reduction of esters and amides:
 - Esters: <span class="reaction-eq">R-COOR' + 4[H] ⟶ R-CH2OH + R'-OH</span> (LiAlH4)
 - Amides: <span class="reaction-eq">R-CONH2 + 4[H] ⟶ R-CH2NH2 + H2O</span> (LiAlH4, forms 1° Amine)
- d. Reaction of Grignard reagent with esters: <span class="reaction-eq">H-COOR' + 2R-Mg-X ⟶ 2° Alcohol</span> | <span class="reaction-eq">R-COOR' + 2R''-Mg-X ⟶ 3° Alcohol</span>

## High-Yield Summary Tables & Concepts ( Extras)

### 1. Functional Group Identification Tests
| Test Name | Reagent | Positive Result (Observation) | Compounds that test Positive |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lucas Test** | ZnCl2 + conc. HCl | Cloudiness/Turbidity | 3° (immediate), 2° (5 mins), 1° (no rxn at RT) |
| **Iodoform (Haloform) Test** | I2 + NaOH | Yellow ppt (CHI3) | Compounds with CH3-CO- or CH3-CH(OH)- groups (e.g., Ethanol, Acetaldehyde, Acetone) |
| **Tollen's Test** | Ammoniacal AgNO3 | Silver Mirror | All Aldehydes (Aliphatic & Aromatic) |
| **Fehling's/Benedict's** | Cu(OH)2 + NaOH | Red ppt (Cu2O) | Aliphatic Aldehydes only |
| **2,4-DNPH Test** | 2,4-Dinitrophenylhydrazine | Yellow/Orange ppt | All Aldehydes & Ketones |
| **Baeyer's Test** | Cold, dil. alkaline KMnO4 | Purple color decolorizes | Alkenes and Alkynes (Unsaturation) |
| **Bromine Water** | Br2 in CCl4 | Orange-red color decolorizes | Alkenes, Alkynes |

### 2. Crucial Reaction Rules & Mechanisms
- **Markovnikov's Rule:** In the addition of HX to an asymmetrical alkene, the hydrogen attaches to the carbon with more hydrogens.
- **Zaitsev's Rule:** During dehydrohalogenation (elimination), the more substituted (more stable) alkene is the major product.
- **Electrophilic Aromatic Substitution (EAS):** 
 - *Nitration:* NO2+ (Nitronium ion)
 - *Sulfonation:* SO3 (Sulfur trioxide)
 - *Halogenation:* Cl+ / Br+ (Halonium ion)
 - *Alkylation/Acylation:* R+ / RCO+ (Carbocation/Acylium ion)
- **Directing Groups in Benzene:**
 - **Ortho/Para Directors (Activating):** -OH, -NH2, -CH3, -OR. (Exceptions: Halogens are o/p directors but *deactivating*).
 - **Meta Directors (Deactivating):** -NO2, -COOH, -CHO, -CN, -SO3H.

### 3. Acidic & Basic Trends (Frequently Tested)
- **Acidity Order:** Carboxylic Acid > Phenol > Water > Alcohol > Terminal Alkyne
- **Reactivity towards Nucleophilic Addition (Aldehydes/Ketones):** Formaldehyde > Acetaldehyde > Acetone
- **Reactivity of Alkyl Halides (SN1):** 3° > 2° > 1° > CH3X *(Favors tertiary, weak nucleophile, polar protic solvent, racemate)*
- **Reactivity of Alkyl Halides (SN2):** CH3X > 1° > 2° > 3° *(Favors primary, strong nucleophile, polar aprotic solvent, inversion)*
- **Reactivity of Carboxylic Acid Derivatives:** Acyl Halide > Acid Anhydride > Ester > Amide
- **Leaving Group Ability:** I⁻ > Br⁻ > Cl⁻ > F⁻

### 4. Named Reactions to Memorize
- **Wurtz Synthesis:** 2RX + 2Na ⟶ R-R + 2NaX (Alkanes prep)
- **Sabatier-Senderens:** Hydrogenation of alkenes using Ni at 250°C
- **Friedel-Crafts:** Alkylation/Acylation of Benzene using AlCl3 catalyst
- **Darzen's Process:** Alcohol + SOCl2 ⟶ Alkyl Chloride + SO2 + HCl (Best prep method)
- **Williamson Synthesis:** R-ONa + R'-X ⟶ R-O-R' (Ether prep)
- **Dow's Process:** Chlorobenzene + NaOH (360°C, 150atm) ⟶ Phenol
- **Aldol Condensation:** Aldehydes/Ketones with alpha-H + dil. NaOH ⟶ Beta-hydroxy aldehyde/ketone
- **Cannizzaro Reaction:** Aldehydes with NO alpha-H + conc. NaOH ⟶ Alcohol + Carboxylic acid salt (Disproportionation)
- **Clemmensen Reduction:** Aldehyde/Ketone + Zn-Hg/HCl ⟶ Alkane
- **Wolff-Kishner Reduction:** Aldehyde/Ketone + NH2NH2/KOH ⟶ Alkane
- **Esterification:** Carboxylic Acid + Alcohol + H2SO4 ⇌ Ester + Water
