## BIO-10 — Respiration & Gas Exchange

---

⭐ **Q69.** Which of the following processes occurs in the cytosol of the cell?
- A. Kreb's Cycle
- B. Electron Transport Chain
- C. Glycolysis
**Answer: C** [Official Sample]

---

**Q70.** [ 2012] In aerobic cellular respiration, most ATPs are synthesized during:
- A. Glycolysis
- B. Oxidation of pyruvic acid
- C. The Krebs cycle
- D. Electron transport chain
**Answer: D**

---

🔥 **Q71.** [ 2024] [REPEATED 3x] Cellular respiration is essential due to:
- A. To make ATP
- B. To get rid of CO₂
- C. To make NADPH
- D. To use ATP
**Answer: A**

---

**Q72.** [ 2012] Which respiratory organ is NOT closely associated with blood supply?
- A. Lungs in vertebrates
- B. Gills in fish
- C. Tracheal tubules in insects
- D. Skin in earthworm
**Answer: C**

---

🔥 **Q73.** [ 2024] [REPEATED 2x] Lungs exchange gases with blood through:
- A. Alveoli
- B. Bronchi
- C. Trachea
- D. Larynx
**Answer: A**
