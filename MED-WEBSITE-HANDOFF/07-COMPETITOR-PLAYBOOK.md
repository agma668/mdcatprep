# 07 — COMPETITOR PLAYBOOK (learn from them, then beat them)

Direct study of the two academies you named, done 2026-09-22, plus the
competitor snapshot the cadet site's SEO audit built. Their pages were read in
full; here's what each does well, what they lack, and the features to copy.

## 1. sirmunironlineacademy.com/preparation — the "complete ecosystem" pitch

What they do well:
- **One promise above the fold**: "Everything you need to clear the [exam] —
  practice quizzes, study notes, past papers, admission alerts, expert
  guidance", with dual CTAs (Start Free Quiz / Read Guide).
- **Six-pillar toolkit grid**: Practice Quizzes · Study Notes · Past Papers ·
  Admission Calendar · Expert Guidance · Progress Tracking — each with a
  two-sentence benefit, not a feature list.
- **Trust section with hard numbers**: "1000+ students coached, 99% success
  rate, placed in 15+ colleges" (whether or not the numbers are real, the
  STRUCTURE converts — use it only with honest numbers).
- **"Built for serious aspirants" narrative blocks**: exam-focused curriculum,
  proven track record, complete ecosystem — three long paragraphs selling the
  system, not the features.
- **Personal brand**: everything is "Sir Munir" — a face and a name. People
  trust people. (MED has a founder page; keep surfacing it.)
- **Eligibility calculators** (age + BMI) as linkable tools.

What they lack (your wedge):
- No llms.txt, no visible FAQ schema, not answer-blocked, no interactive
  practice on the page (it's a brochure), no live stats, no community surface.

## 2. shaheenacademy.com/entrytestcaded — the dates page + pattern clarity

What they do well:
- **The exam pattern stated plainly**: which subjects the tests carry (Urdu,
  English, Maths, Science, Islamiat) and that most colleges test the same five.
- **THE DATES LIST** — expected entry-test dates for ~20 colleges with exact
  days. This is their single strongest asset: it ranks, it gets shared in
  WhatsApp groups, and parents return to it. It's just a well-maintained table.
- Age criteria per college type ("PAF 11.5–13.5, Military 12–14") in one line.
- Interview-prep angle ("interview preparatory classes") — the exam after the
  exam, which most competitors ignore.
- Session calendar ("class session starts in April") — creates urgency.

What they lack: no practice engine, no notes library, no analytics, dates
inside a long page rather than a dedicated yearly page, nothing for AI engines.

## 3. What to build on the MED site (priority order)

1. **The dates page** (`/mdcat-test-dates-2027`): a table of every MDCAT
   registry's expected registration window + test window (UHS, KMU, SZABMU,
   DUHS, BUMHS, NUMS, ETEA…), refreshed as announcements land, with a
   "last verified" stamp. Add to SEO_PAGES + catalog + IndexNow. This is the
   cheapest, highest-yield page in this niche.
2. **Toolkit grid on the landing** — six honest pillars with MDCAT content
   (MCQ Practice · Master Notes · Mock Tests · Test Calendar · Analytics ·
   Community). Copy Sir Munir's structure, keep every number honest.
3. **Interactive demo MCQ on the landing** (the cadet `MCQDemoSection`) — a
   playable question beats any paragraph.
4. **Per-registry pages** (`/uhs`, `/kmu`, `/szabmu`, `/duhs`, `/bumhs`,
   `/nums`, `/etea`): pattern, syllabus summary, dates, FAQ schema, practice
   CTA. Each is its own search term — this mirrors the cadet site's
   one-page-per-college directory, which is its deepest SEO moat.
5. **Eligibility / aggregate calculator** as a linkable tool (Sir Munir's
   calculators + the cadet site's "linkable assets" plan). For MDCAT: an
   aggregate calculator (MDCAT %, FSc %, Hifz/equations per registry rules).
6. **Interview/guidance content**: "MDCAT day: what actually happens",
   "how merit lists work", "private vs regular candidate".
7. **Answer-block every guide** (40–60 word direct answer up top) + FAQPage
   schema — the AI-citation layer none of these competitors has.

## 4. Voice & positioning

- **Free is the wedge** — every competitor monetizes; the cadet site's brand
  chip says "100% Free — No hidden charges" and brass/reserved typography makes
  "free" feel like insignia. Keep it (MED is free too).
- **Honest numbers beat big numbers**: live counts with an "as of" stamp beat
  unverifiable "99% success rate" claims — and can't be called out.
- **Name a real human.** Founder page, founder link in the hero. Trust is the
  product in this market.
- Copy rule inherited from the cadet founder: never promise admission
  outcomes; promise preparation. "Get into your dream cadet college" works as
  an aspirational CTA; a success-rate percentage does not.
