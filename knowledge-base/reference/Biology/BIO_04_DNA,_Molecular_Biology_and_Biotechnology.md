🔥 **Q33.** [ 2012] A mRNA is 999 nucleotides long. The number of amino acids in the polypeptide chain will be:
- A. 111
- B. 333
- C. 666
- D. 1998
**Answer: B** (999/3 = 333)

---

🔥 **Q34.** [ 2022] A polypeptide consists of 250 amino acids. How many nucleotides in the mRNA?
- A. 200
- B. 250
- C. 450
- D. 750
**Answer: D** (250 × 3 = 750)
