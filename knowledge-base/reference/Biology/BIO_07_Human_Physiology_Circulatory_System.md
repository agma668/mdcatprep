🔥 **Q55.** [ 2021] What is the correct pathway of blood from heart to lungs?
- A. Left atrium → left ventricle → lungs → right ventricles → right atrium
- B. Right atrium → left atrium → right ventricle → left ventricle → lungs
- C. Right atrium → right ventricle → lungs → left ventricle → left atrium
- D. Right atrium → right ventricle → lungs → left atrium → left ventricle
**Answer: D**

---

🔥 **Q56.** [ 2024] [REPEATED 2x] Path of blood from lower body to the heart and then to the nephron:
- A. Right atrium → pulmonary artery → pulmonary vein → left ventricle → aorta → renal artery
**Answer: A**

🔥 **Q57.** [ 2024] [REPEATED 2x] Common in both arteries and capillaries:

**Q59.** [ 2024] Which is NOT a similarity between hemoglobin and myoglobin?
- A. 4 Oxygen binding sites
- B. Iron-containing heme group
- C. Hemoglobin is present in muscles
- D. Hemoglobin contains 1 polypeptide chain
**Answer: A** (Hb binds 4 O₂, myoglobin binds only 1)
