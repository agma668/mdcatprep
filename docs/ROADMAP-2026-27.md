## 2. What "students actually pay" requires (no marketing, just product and trust)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

DECISION 2026-10-06 (founder): everything stays free for now and the site says so. Payment work is parked; the rest of this section is the plan for when he reopens it.

The site is free today. People pay when five things are all true. Check them in order; do not charge before the first three pass.

1. Trust: every number on the site is real, every answer key is right, no fake teachers, no invented counts. (Section 5, WS5)
2. Coverage: each portal actually matches its exam (pattern, timing, syllabus, past papers). (Section 3 and 6)
3. A job done: a student can see their weak topics, fix them, and see a mock score move. (WS2)
4. Something worth paying for that free does not give. Hypotheses to TEST, not facts:
   - full-length timed mocks that copy the real paper layout with ranking among real users
   - a weekly plan generated from their diagnostic and the exam date
   - explanations for every past paper question, in English and Urdu
   - offline packs for weak connectivity areas
   - result-day help: merit calculator, district quota view, choice-filling guidance
5. A payment path that works in Pakistan. Stripe is not available to Pakistani merchants. Candidates to evaluate: JazzCash, Easypaisa,
   Raast QR, bank transfer, local gateways such as Safepay or PayFast. Merchant onboarding usually asks for a registered business or NTN.
   VERIFY before building. MVP can be manual: pay by transfer, send receipt on WhatsApp, founder flips a flag in the admin page.

Proposed gates before turning payment on (these are my proposals, the founder can change them):
- Content audit passes: every portal claim on the site is backed by a counted file (WS3).
- At least 200 real weekly active students and a measured week-1 return rate (we do not know either today).
- 20 student conversations where the student says what they would pay for and how much. Price comes from this, not from a guess.
- A refund rule written in plain words and a working way to contact us.

## 4. Phased plan with exit gates
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Dates assume today is 6 Oct 2026. Every phase ends with a gate; do not start the next phase's big work until the gate passes.
Every content wave goes through the existing gate ladder (tsc, eslint on touched files, next build, live smoke at 390/768/desktop, one commit).

### Phase 0: honest foundation (6 Oct to 13 Oct)
- Visual QA the already-live landing at 390x844 and desktop, fix what looks wrong, commit and push.
- Replace the live FAQ pattern answer with the official PMDC 2026/27 notice figures; fix "7 Subjects", "live WhatsApp classes" and the stray "Loading..." text.
- Replace hand-typed portal counts in exams-config with counts computed from the actual banks (one source of truth).
- Remove or relabel every unbacked portal promise (NUMS psych, Balochistan/FATA tabs, GIKI, LUMS) until content exists.
- Founder decides the "900 students" floor (recommend: show real numbers only; hide the tile until it is meaningful).
- Founder verifies: HEC B&F test date, NUST NET-1 dates, and attaches a real domain (canonical now points at the vercel URL).
- Confirm main is the deploy branch and fix AGENTS.md and the board.
GATE: no number on any public page that is not computed from data; landing live; domain decision made.

### Phase 1: NUST NET sprint plus medical result-season funnel (13 Oct to 17 Nov)
Why: NET-1 test starts 17 Nov and registration closes 14 Nov. Medical result and merit season is happening now.
- NET portal minimum viable: official NET pattern page (verify from NUST), timed CBT-style simulator, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry or CS,
  English and IQ banks written from the NUST syllabus. Target is a number we report honestly each wave; do not pad.
- Medical result-season tools: aggregate calculator (10% matric, 40% FSc, 50% MDCAT per the standard formula), pass-mark check (55%/50%),
  "what next" page: merit lists, re-sit plan. Add a repeater onboarding path.
- Signup captures the fields in section 1.
- Past papers: finish UHS 2024 transcription as the template, build board > year > paper > subject browsing and a practice mode for keyed papers.
GATE: NET portal has a working timed simulator and a counted verified bank; a repeater can go from result to a plan in under 5 minutes.

### Phase 2: HEC Balochistan/FATA module and quality pass (17 Nov to 31 Dec)
- HEC aptitude test: confirm the pattern from the official notice, then build verbal, quantitative and analytical reasoning practice with a timed mode.
  Note USAT category tables list 75-MCQ papers with verbal and quantitative blocks; do not assume B&F uses the same until verified.
- Balochistan tools: district and divisional quota explainer, reserved-seat explainer (HEC seats, BMC, KMU merged-district seats), BUMHS timeline.
  Needs the current BUMHS prospectus; build only from it.
- Low-bandwidth and offline mode for practice (service worker exists, test it on slow 3G).
- Urdu support for navigation and explanations on the highest-traffic pages.
- Payment MVP (manual flow) behind a flag, only if the gates in section 2 pass.
GATE: portal claims all match files; slow-3G smoke passes; payment flag exists but is off.

### Phase 3: MDCAT 2027 cohort programme (Jan to Mar 2027)
- Full syllabus map: every PMDC syllabus topic linked to notes, topical MCQs, flashcards and past-paper questions; show coverage gaps honestly.
- Full-length mock series that mirrors the real paper (180 MCQs, 3 hours, subject split per the PMDC notice, no negative marking), with analytics.
- Past papers complete for every legally usable sitting, with keys and explanations.
- GIKI, FAST and LUMS content ready before their spring cycles open.
- Weekly study plan engine from diagnostic plus exam date.
GATE: a student can follow a 20-week plan end to end inside the site; weekly actives and week-1 return rate are measured.

### Phase 4: peak season and charging (Apr to Aug 2027)
- Turn payments on if gates held. Price from interviews. Refund rule published.
- Parent progress view. Admin tools for support.
- Performance and load work for peak traffic (PMDC registration and result days).
- Post-test retrospective: compare our mock scores with real results from willing students to prove or disprove that the product moves scores.

### WS4 Content pipeline (this is where wrong answers can hurt a student)
- Sources allowed: the PMDC/NUMS syllabus and official notices; officially released papers from examining bodies; government textbooks for facts (write
  ORIGINAL questions, never paste a book's exercise questions); the founder's own notes; compilations the founder has cleared in writing.
- Sources not allowed: any AKU material (founder ruling), academy banks pasted verbatim, anything watermarked by a coaching brand republished as ours.
  Source line on every item names the examining body or "original", never a website watermark.
- Photo and scan ingestion: use page-to-image reading (works well, proven on UHS 2024), output structured JSON, flag low-confidence items.
  Tesseract-style OCR is not reliable on these scans.
- Every item has status draft -> verified. Only verified ships. Answer keys are cross-checked against an official key or a second independent pass.
- Dedupe against existing banks by normalised question text before ingest. Keep key integrity (question_key unique).
- Tag past_paper_exam / year / section only when the file proves it.
- Waves of 200 to 400, report real counts, never pad. JARVIS standing mission (~2,000 MDCAT/NUMS questions) uses this same pipeline; no duplicate ingestion.
- Past paper section spec: board > year > code > subject, key status shown on the card ("official key", "key circulates, unofficial", "no key"),
  questions without a key are readable but not scored.
- Pilot first: one chapter, ~20 pages, measure transcription error rate before any bulk run.

## 7. Risks
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Wrong answer keys: one bad key destroys trust. Verification states are non-negotiable.
- Copyright takedown: keep the source rules in WS4; keep a visible contact.
- Claims drift: portals promised more than they hold. Fix in Phase 0 and keep counts computed.
- Disruptions in Quetta: tests and internet get postponed. Offline mode and graceful dates matter.
- Agent collisions: many agents, one repo. Claim files on the board; never git add .
- Stale facts: exam dates move (MDCAT 2026 moved once, NUMS twice). Show "last verified" dates and keep a dated fact sheet.
- Scope: this is a lot. If time runs short, cut in this order: Urdu, parent view, LUMS/FAST/ECAT, then GIKI. Do not cut verification.
