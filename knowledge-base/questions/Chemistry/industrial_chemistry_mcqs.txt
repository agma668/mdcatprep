# Most Important Industrial Chemistry MCQs with Answers

## Industrial Chemistry MCQs with Answers

Which component in a dye is primarily responsible for its ability to bind to a fabric?

Auxochromes are functional groups that intensify color and also help the dye to fix onto the fabric by interacting with it.

Bismarck Brown, used for dyeing wool and in boot polishes, belongs to which class of dyes based on its structure?

Bismarck Brown contains two azo groups (-N=N-) linking three aromatic rings, making it an azo dye.

What is the color of the indicator phenolphthalein in a basic medium?

In a basic solution, the structure of phenolphthalein changes to a quinoid form, which imparts a distinct red or pink color.

Vat dyes, such as Indigo, are applied to fabric in a special way. What is the key first step in the vat dyeing process?

Vat dyes are insoluble, so they must be chemically reduced (often with sodium hydrosulphide) to a soluble, colorless form that can penetrate the fibers.

Malachite Green is an example of a triarylmethane dye. What is the characteristic structural feature of this class?

This accurately describes the core structure of triarylmethane dyes, where a central carbon is bonded to three aromatic rings, one of which is in the quinoid form.

Which type of dye is applied to a fabric with the help of salts of aluminum or chromium, which form an insoluble complex?

Mordants are metallic salts that are applied to the fabric to form a coordination complex, which in turn binds the dye to the fabric.

Congo Red is prepared by coupling tetrazotised benzidine with two molecules of which compound?

The structure of Congo Red confirms that two molecules of naphthionic acid are coupled with the tetrazotised benzidine.

Azoic dyeing, also known as ingrain dyeing, is a method that involves:

In this process, the fabric is soaked in a coupling agent and then in a solution of a diazotized amine, forming the final dye 'in-grain' (inside the fabric).

Which of the following is an example of a Nitro dye, containing the -NO₂ group as a chromophore?

The structure of Naphthol Yellow S clearly shows nitro groups attached to the naphthalene ring system.

The primary chromophore in azo dyes is the:

This diazo linkage (-N=N-) is the defining chromophore for the entire class of azo dyes.

The polymerization of ethylene to form polyethylene is an example of what type of polymerization?

In this reaction, ethylene monomers add to each other in such a way that the polymer contains all the atoms of the monomer unit. The double bond breaks to form new single bonds.

What is the defining characteristic of a condensation polymer?

This is the hallmark of condensation polymerization, where monomers join together by 'condensing out' a small molecule, such as water.

Nylon-6,6 is a polyamide formed by the condensation reaction between which two monomers?

Nylon-6,6 derives its name from its two six-carbon monomers: adipic acid (a dicarboxylic acid) and hexamethylene diamine (a diamine).

Which of the following is a key characteristic of a thermosetting polymer?

Heating causes extensive cross-linking between polymer chains, forming a rigid, infusible solid that cannot be softened by reheating.

Polyvinyl chloride (PVC) is an addition polymer formed from the monomer vinyl chloride, which is synthesized from acetylene and HCl.

Vinyl chloride consists of a two-carbon alkene (ethene) with one chlorine atom attached, making its IUPAC name chloroethene.

The reaction by which monomers are converted into polymers is known as:

This is the general term for the process of joining many small molecules (monomers) together to form a very large molecule (polymer).

A polymer synthesized from more than one kind of monomer is called a:

The prefix 'co-' indicates that it is formed from two or more different monomers working together.

During the addition polymerization of an alkene monomer, what happens to its double bond?

The electrons from the weaker pi bond are used to form the strong sigma bonds that constitute the polymer chain.

Which of the following is a common use for PVC?

PVC is a versatile and durable plastic used in a wide range of building and consumer products, including floor covering, roofing, and imitation leather.

A polymer that softens on heating and becomes hard again on cooling is known as:

The prefix 'thermo-' refers to heat, and 'plastic' refers to its ability to be molded. These polymers can be repeatedly softened by heat and hardened by cooling.

Adhesives that are made from sources like vegetable matter, starch, or animal skin are classified as:

These adhesives are derived from biological or inorganic mineral sources and are often referred to as bioadhesives.

How do drying adhesives, such as white glue and rubber cement, harden?

These adhesives consist of a polymer dissolved or suspended in a solvent. The bond forms as the solvent evaporates.

What is the key application requirement for contact adhesives?

Contact adhesives form a bond upon contact after the solvent has evaporated from both coated surfaces.

Hot glue, which is applied with a glue gun, is an example of which type of adhesive?

Also known as hot melt adhesives, these are thermoplastics that are melted, applied, and then harden as they cool to room temperature.

Elastomers, thermoplastics, and thermosetting adhesives are all examples of what broad category of adhesives?

These are all classifications of adhesives created through chemical synthesis.

UV and light curing adhesives are composed of low or medium molecular weight:

These adhesives consist essentially of low or medium molecular weight resins that polymerize upon exposure to light.

What is the primary function of an adhesive?

This is the fundamental definition of an adhesive – a compound that adheres or bonds two items together.

An example of a natural adhesive derived from starch is:

Dextrin is a type of adhesive made from starch, which is a natural vegetable matter.

Which type of adhesive is popular for crafts due to its ease of use with a wide range of materials?

Hot melt adhesives have become popular for crafts because of their ease of use and the wide range of common materials to which they can adhere.

The term 'bioadhesives' often refers to which category of adhesives?

Natural adhesives derived from biological sources are often referred to as bioadhesives.

The preparation of Methyl Orange involves diazotising which compound first?

The synthesis of Methyl Orange starts with the diazotization of sulphanilic acid.

Which of the following dyes is also well-known as an acid-base indicator?

Although classified as a triarylmethane dye, phenolphthalein is better known for its use as an acid-base indicator.

Disperse dyes are primarily used for which type of fabric?

Disperse dyes are used with modern synthetic fabrics such as nylon, orlon, polyester, and cellulose acetate.

The para quinoid structure is the characteristic chromophore in which class of dyes?

The para quinoid chromophore is present in anthracene-type dyes, such as Alizarin.

What is the function of an auxochrome like -OH or -NH₂ in a dye molecule?

The polar auxochrome makes the dye water-soluble and binds it to the fabric by interacting with oppositely charged groups.

Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of an addition polymer?

Addition polymerization is a chain-growth process. Step-growth is characteristic of condensation polymerization.

The monomers adipic acid and hexamethylene diamine each contain how many carbon atoms?

Nylon-6,6 derives its name from its two starting materials, both of which have six carbons.

What type of chemical bond is formed between the monomers in Nylon-6,6?

Nylon-6,6 is a polyamide, formed by the reaction between a carboxylic acid and an amine to create an amide linkage (-CONH-).

A polymer made from a single type of monomer, such as polyethylene from ethylene, is called a:

Polymers which are synthesized from only one kind of monomer are called homopolymers.

The synthesis of vinyl chloride monomer can be achieved by reacting acetylene with which substance in the presence of HgCl₂?

Vinyl chloride is obtained from acetylene by treatment with HCl in the presence of HgCl₂.

Which of these is NOT a typical use for Nylon-6,6?

Nylon-6,6 is used for fibers, filaments, bristles, and molded objects. Floor covering and gramophone records are uses for PVC.

The primary difference between a thermoplastic and a thermosetting polymer lies in their:

A thermoplastic softens on heating, while a thermosetting polymer becomes hard on heating and cannot be softened again.

Addition polymerization is also known as:

Addition polymers are also referred to as Chain-Growth Polymers.

Condensation polymerization is also known as:

Condensation polymers are also referred to as step-growth polymers.

Which of these adhesive types works by a physical process rather than a chemical one?

Hot melt adhesives are thermoplastics that are applied hot and simply harden as they cool, which is a physical change of state.

Which category of adhesives requires application to both surfaces before they are joined?

Contact adhesives must be applied to both surfaces and allowed some time to dry before the two surfaces are pushed together.

An adhesive made from inorganic mineral sources would be classified as:

Natural adhesives are made from inorganic mineral sources or biological sources.

White glue, a common household and school adhesive, belongs to which class?

White glue and rubber cements are members of the drying adhesive family, which harden as their solvent evaporates.

What is a major advantage of modern synthetic adhesives over many traditional natural adhesives?

Some modern adhesives are extremely strong and are becoming increasingly important in modern construction and industry.

The process of using a glue gun involves which type of adhesive?

The glue gun melts the solid hot melt adhesive and allows the liquid to pass through onto the material where it solidifies.

Mordant Green 4 is a dye that contains which chromophore?

The -NO group is the chromophore in Nitroso dyes, and Mordant Green 4 is given as an example.

Para Red was the first azo dye prepared. Its synthesis involves the reaction of diazotized p-nitroaniline with what coupling compound?

Para red is obtained by the reaction of diazotized p-nitroaniline with β-naphthol.

The color of Methyl Orange changes from yellow in base to red in acid due to:

The change in color of Methyl Orange is due to the change in the structure of the ions.

What happens to the colorless, soluble form of Indigo ('Indigo White') when the dyed cloth is hung in the air?

When the cloth is hung in air, the colorless compound is oxidized back to the insoluble colored dye.

The synthesis of phenolphthalein involves heating phthalic anhydride with two moles of what substance?

Phenolphthalein is prepared by heating phthalic anhydride (1 mole) and phenol (two moles).

Direct dyes, like Martius Yellow, can be applied directly to fibers like wool and silk because:

A direct dye contains an acidic or basic auxochrome which combines with the opposite polar group present in the chemical structure of the fiber.

Which of the following is NOT a main type of dye when classified by structure according to the provided text?

Azo, Anthraquinone, and Indigo dyes are all classifications by structure. Vat Dyes are a classification based on the method of application.

The chemical stability of a dye, meaning it won't wash out with soap or fade in sunlight, is referred to as its:

The dye must be 'fast' or chemically stable so that the color will not wash out with soap and water, or fade on exposure to sunlight.

The structure of Alizarin is based on which parent aromatic hydrocarbon?

Alizarin is a typical anthraquinone dye, which is an anthracene-type dye.

The 'lake' formed during mordant dyeing is:

The mordant forms an insoluble coordination complex between the fiber and the dye, which appears in the form of lakes.

What is the fundamental difference between homopolymers and copolymers?

Homopolymers are synthesized from only one kind of monomer, whereas copolymers are prepared from more than one kind.

The process of forming polyethylene can be catalyzed by Ziegler catalysts, which consist of trialkyl aluminum and what other compound?

Ziegler Catalysts consist of a trialkyl aluminum and titanium tetrachloride.

In the abbreviated formula for an addition polymer like polyethylene, -[CH₂-CH₂]-n, what does the 'n' represent?

'n' is the degree of polymerization, indicating that the monomer unit in the brackets is repeated many times to form the long chain polymer.

Which of these is NOT a condensation polymer?

PVC is formed by the addition polymerization of vinyl chloride monomers. Nylon and polyesters are explicitly mentioned as condensation polymers.

The key difference in the final structure of thermosetting polymers compared to thermoplastic polymers is the presence of:

Thermosetting polymers become hard on heating and cannot be softened, which is due to the formation of a rigid, cross-linked 3D network structure.

To create a thermosetting polymer, it is beneficial to use:

To form a cross-linked network instead of linear chains, at least one of the monomers must have more than two functional groups to allow for branching. This leads to a thermoset structure.

What is the structural formula of adipic acid, a monomer of Nylon-6,6?

Adipic acid is listed as a reactant for Nylon-6,6 with the formula HOOC(CH₂)₄COOH.

What is the structural formula of hexamethylene diamine, a monomer of Nylon-6,6?

Hexamethylene diamine is listed as a reactant for Nylon-6,6 with the formula H₂N(CH₂)₆NH₂.

The repeating unit in polyethylene is:

The ethylene monomer (CH₂=CH₂) polymerizes to form the repeating unit -CH₂-CH₂-.

The repeating unit in polyvinyl chloride is:

The vinyl chloride monomer (CH₂=CHCl) polymerizes to form the repeating unit -CH₂-CH-Cl.

An adhesive that is a thermoplastic applied in a molten state is known as a:

'Hot melt' adhesives are thermoplastics that are applied hot and allowed to harden as they cool.

Which of the following is an example of a synthetic adhesive?

Elastomers, thermoplastics, and thermosetting adhesives are examples of synthetic adhesives.

The hardening of rubber cement is an example of which process?

Rubber cement is a member of the drying adhesive family, which hardens as the solvent evaporates.

Which of these is NOT listed as a category of synthetic adhesives?

Elastomers, thermoplastics, and thermosetting adhesives are listed as synthetic. Bioadhesives are referred to as natural adhesives.

What is the key functional group that gives an auxochrome its ability to bind to a protein fiber like wool?

The polar auxochrome binds the dye to the fabric by interaction with the oppositely charged groups of the fiber structure, such as the -NH₂ group of wool.

The reaction to form Nylon-6,6 eliminates a small molecule. What is this molecule?

Condensation polymers are formed with the elimination of simple molecules such as H₂O. The reaction image for Nylon-6,6 explicitly shows -H₂O.

If you mix two different types of monomers, one with two acid groups and one with two alcohol groups, what type of polymer will you form?

Polyesters are a main type of condensation polymer. The reaction between an acid and an alcohol forms an ester linkage. Since two different monomers are used, it will be a copolymer.

The main purpose of a mordant in dyeing is to:

The mordant forms an insoluble coordination complex between the fiber and the dye and binds the two.
