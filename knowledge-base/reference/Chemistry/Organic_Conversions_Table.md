# Organic Chemistry: The Ultimate Conversion Tables 🔄
*(A comprehensive cheat sheet for every functional group reaction and conversion)*

These tables cover **ALL** the aliphatic and aromatic conversions required for the Entrance Test, formatted specifically to help you memorize "Reactant + Reagent ➔ Product". 

<br>

## 1. OXIDATION REACTIONS (Adding Oxygen / Removing Hydrogen)
| Starting Compound (Reactant) | Reagent & Conditions | Final Product | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Alcohol** (`R-CH2OH`) | `K2Cr2O7` or `KMnO4` + `H2SO4`, **Distil** | **Aldehyde** (`R-CHO`) | Distil immediately to prevent further oxidation. |
| **Primary Alcohol** (`R-CH2OH`) | `K2Cr2O7` or `KMnO4` + `H2SO4`, **Reflux** | **Carboxylic Acid** (`R-COOH`) | Complete oxidation. |
| **Secondary Alcohol** (`R-CH(OH)-R'`) | `K2Cr2O7` or `KMnO4` + `H2SO4`, Reflux | **Ketone** (`R-CO-R'`) | Stops at ketone. Hard to oxidize further. |
| **Tertiary Alcohol** | Oxidizing agents | **No Reaction** | Resists oxidation. |
| **Aldehyde** (`R-CHO`) | `K2Cr2O7` or Tollens' / Fehling's | **Carboxylic Acid** (`R-COOH`) | Readily oxidized. |
| **Ketone** (`R-CO-R'`) | Normal Oxidizing Agents | **No Reaction** | Needs very strong, drastic conditions to break C-C bonds. |
| **Alkene** (`C=C`) | Cold, dilute alkaline `KMnO4` | **Diol** (e.g., Ethane-1,2-diol) | Baeyer's Test (purple to brown). |
| **Alkene** (`C=C`) | `O3` (Ozone) then `Zn/H2O` | **Aldehydes / Ketones** | Ozonolysis (cleaves the double bond). |
| **Alkylbenzene** (e.g., Toluene) | `KMnO4` + `H2SO4`, Heat | **Benzoic Acid** | Any length carbon chain turns into a -COOH group! |

## 2. REDUCTION REACTIONS (Adding Hydrogen / Removing Oxygen)
| Starting Compound (Reactant) | Reagent & Conditions | Final Product | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Carboxylic Acid** (`R-COOH`) | `LiAlH4` in dry ether | **Primary Alcohol** (`R-CH2OH`) | Strong reducing agent required. (`NaBH4` will not work). |
| **Aldehyde** (`R-CHO`) | `NaBH4` or `LiAlH4` or `H2/Ni` | **Primary Alcohol** (`R-CH2OH`) | Readily reduced. |
| **Ketone** (`R-CO-R'`) | `NaBH4` or `LiAlH4` or `H2/Ni` | **Secondary Alcohol** | Readily reduced. |
| **Aldehyde / Ketone** | `Zn-Hg` + conc. `HCl` | **Alkane** (`R-CH3`) | **Clemmensen Reduction** (Removes Oxygen entirely). |
| **Aldehyde / Ketone** | `N2H4` + `KOH`, heat | **Alkane** (`R-CH3`) | **Wolff-Kishner Reduction**. |
| **Nitrile** (`R-C≡N`) | `LiAlH4` or `H2/Ni` | **Primary Amine** (`R-CH2NH2`) | Adds 4 hydrogens. |
| **Amide** (`R-CONH2`) | `LiAlH4` | **Primary Amine** (`R-CH2NH2`) | Converts C=O to CH2. |
| **Nitrobenzene** | `Sn` + `HCl` (or `Fe` + `HCl`) | **Phenylamine (Aniline)** | Reduction of nitro group. |

## 3. SUBSTITUTION & ELIMINATION (Alkyl Halides & Alcohols)
| Starting Compound | Reagent & Conditions | Final Product | Reaction Type |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Alkane** | `Cl2` or `Br2` + UV Light | **Alkyl Halide** (`R-Cl`) | Free Radical Substitution. |
| **Alkyl Halide** (`R-X`) | **Aqueous** `NaOH` or `KOH`, heat | **Alcohol** (`R-OH`) | Nucleophilic Substitution (SN1/SN2). |
| **Alkyl Halide** (`R-X`) | **Alcoholic** `KOH`, heat | **Alkene** (`C=C`) | Elimination (E1/E2). Removes HX. |
| **Alkyl Halide** (`R-X`) | `KCN` in ethanol, reflux | **Nitrile** (`R-C≡N`) | Adds a Carbon! Great for step-up reactions. |
| **Alkyl Halide** (`R-X`) | Excess `NH3` in ethanol, heat | **Primary Amine** (`R-NH2`) | Nucleophilic Substitution. |
| **Alcohol** (`R-OH`) | `SOCl2` (Thionyl chloride) | **Alkyl Halide** (`R-Cl`) | Best method (gases SO2 and HCl escape). |
| **Alcohol** (`R-OH`) | `PCl5` or `PCl3` or `HX` | **Alkyl Halide** (`R-Cl`) | Substitution. |
| **Alcohol** (`R-OH`) | conc. `H2SO4` or `Al2O3`, 170°C | **Alkene** (`C=C`) | Dehydration (Elimination). |

## 4. CARBOXYLIC ACID DERIVATIVES (Esters, Amides, Acyl Chlorides)
| Starting Compounds | Reagent & Conditions | Final Product | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Carboxylic Acid + Alcohol** | conc. `H2SO4`, reflux | **Ester** + `H2O` | **Esterification**. Reversible reaction. |
| **Ester** | Dilute acid (`H+`) + `H2O`, reflux | **Acid + Alcohol** | Acidic Hydrolysis. |
| **Ester** | Dilute alkali (`NaOH`), reflux | **Sodium Salt of Acid + Alcohol** | Saponification (Base Hydrolysis). |
| **Carboxylic Acid** | `PCl5` or `SOCl2` | **Acyl Chloride** (`R-COCl`) | Very reactive intermediate. |
| **Acyl Chloride + Alcohol** | Room temperature | **Ester** + `HCl` | Faster and irreversible way to make esters! |
| **Acyl Chloride + NH3** | Room temperature | **Primary Amine** (`R-CONH2`) | Vigorous reaction. |
| **Carboxylic Acid + NH3** | Heat | **Amide** (`R-CONH2`) + `H2O` | Requires strong heating. |
| **Nitrile** (`R-C≡N`) | Dilute acid (`H+`) + `H2O`, reflux | **Carboxylic Acid** (`R-COOH`) | Acidic Hydrolysis. Very important conversion! |

## 5. ADDITION TO ALKENES
| Starting Compound | Reagent & Conditions | Final Product | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Alkene** | `H2` + `Ni` catalyst, 150°C | **Alkane** | Hydrogenation. |
| **Alkene** | `Br2` in `CCl4` | **Dibromoalkane** | Bromine water test. |
| **Alkene** | `HBr` or `HCl` (Room Temp) | **Alkyl Halide** | Follows **Markovnikov's Rule** (H goes to C with more H's). |
| **Alkene** | `H2O` + conc. `H2SO4` or `H3PO4`, 300°C | **Alcohol** | Hydration of alkenes (Industrial method). |

## 6. BENZENE REACTIONS (Aromatic Chemistry)
| Reactants | Reagent & Conditions (Catalyst) | Product | Reaction Name |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Benzene** + `HNO3` | conc. `H2SO4`, 50-55°C | **Nitrobenzene** | Nitration. |
| **Benzene** + `Cl2` | `FeCl3` or `AlCl3` | **Chlorobenzene** | Halogenation. |
| **Benzene** + `CH3Cl` | `AlCl3` (Anhydrous) | **Methylbenzene (Toluene)** | Friedel-Crafts Alkylation. |
| **Benzene** + `CH3COCl` | `AlCl3` (Anhydrous) | **Acetophenone** | Friedel-Crafts Acylation. |
| **Benzene** + `SO3` | conc. `H2SO4` | **Benzenesulfonic Acid** | Sulfonation. |
