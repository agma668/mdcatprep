⭐ **Q43.** Phospholipids are derivatives of phosphatidic acid. Each molecule consists of:
- A. Two molecules of fatty acid, one molecule of glycerol and one phosphoric acid.
- B. One molecule of fatty acid, two molecules of glycerol and two phosphoric acid.
- C. Two molecules of fatty acid, two molecules of glycerol and one phosphoric acid.
**Answer: A** [Official Sample]
