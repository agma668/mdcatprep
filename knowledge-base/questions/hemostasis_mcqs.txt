A kangaroo rat is an example of an animal exhibiting anhydrobiosis. What is the primary source of the water it uses for survival?

Kangaroo rats derive about 90% of their water from the cellular oxidation of carbohydrates found in the seeds they eat. This characteristic is called anhydrobiosis.

Which of the following nitrogenous wastes requires the most water for safe excretion and is therefore common in aquatic animals?

Ammonia is highly toxic and requires about 500 ml of water per gram of nitrogen to be diluted to a non-toxic level, making it suitable only for animals with constant access to water.

The blood vessel that carries filtered blood away from the glomerulus is the:

The efferent arteriole arises from the glomeruli and carries blood away from it after filtration has occurred.

What causes the high filtration pressure in the glomerulus?

The diameter of the efferent arteriole is about half that of the afferent arteriole, creating a bottleneck that significantly increases blood pressure within the glomerulus, which drives filtration.

A patient is diagnosed with pyelonephritis. Which part of the urinary tract is infected?

Pyelonephritis is the medical term for an infection of the kidneys. An infection of the urethra is urethritis, and an infection of the bladder is cystitis.

The countercurrent multiplier mechanism primarily serves to:

The countercurrent multiplier mechanism, involving the loop of Henle and vasa recta, achieves the reabsorption of about 99.5% of water from the filtrate by creating a concentration gradient.

A patient with chronic kidney failure undergoes haemodialysis. The dialysate fluid used in the dialyzer should contain:

The dialysate absorbs waste by-products from the blood by creating a concentration gradient. To prevent the loss of essential substances, the fluid must have normal concentrations of minerals and electrolytes, but it contains no waste products.

Which of the following is considered a physiological response to hyperthermia (high body temperature)?

When body temperature rises above 37°C (hyperthermia), one of the main physiological responses is sweating. Evaporation of the sweat produces a cooling effect.

A person has a kidney transplant. To prevent the rejection of the new kidney, they are prescribed medications like cyclosporine. What is the primary function of these medications?

Patients are required to take medications such as cyclosporine to suppress their immune system in order to prevent rejection of the transplanted kidney.

The vasa recta are specialized capillaries associated with which part of the nephron?

The vasa recta are a specialized part of the peritubular capillaries that course into the medulla along with the loops of Henle.

The ESWL procedure treats kidney stones by:

In ESWL, an instrument called a lithotripter generates shock waves from outside the patient's body, which are focused on the stone to break it into small pieces.

A person is dehydrated. Which hormonal changes would you expect to see?

During dehydration, anti-diuretic hormone (ADH) promotes water reabsorption from the descending limb and collecting duct, while aldosterone promotes the transport of sodium chloride out of the ascending limb, which also aids water retention.

Tubular secretion is a vital process for:

Tubular secretion is the process by which certain substances, like ammonium and hydrogen ions, are secreted by tubular epithelial cells into the filtrate to maintain the pH of urine.

An animal that produces metabolic heat at a low level and relies on the environment to regulate its body temperature is called a(n):

Ectotherms produce metabolic heat at a low level and rely more on heat derived from the environment to raise their body temperature.

The glomerular filtrate has a chemical composition most similar to:

The glomerular filtrate has a chemical composition similar to that of blood plasma, but it lacks larger molecules like proteins, red blood cells, and platelets.

The functional unit of the kidney is the:

The nephron is the functional unit of the kidney, and it consists of a renal corpuscle and a renal tubule.

Why are urinary tract infections (UTIs) significantly more common in women than in men?

The higher risk of UTIs in women is mostly due to the shortness of the female urethra (1.5 inches compared to 8 inches in men), which allows bacteria to be more easily transferred to the bladder.

A urine test shows a persistently acidic pH. This patient is at a higher risk for which type of kidney stone?

When urine is persistently acidic, there is a higher risk of forming uric acid type stones. Calcium phosphate stones tend to form in alkaline urine.

The thermoregulatory center of the human body, acting as a thermostat, is located in the:

The thermoregulatory centre in the human body is located in the hypothalamus, which acts as a thermostat, detecting the temperature of the blood passing through it.

The primary function that distinguishes juxtamedullary nephrons is their ability to:

Juxtamedullary nephrons have long loops that extend deep into the medulla. This makes juxtamedullary nephrons key for water retention when water is in short supply.

During peritoneal dialysis, where is the dialysate fluid introduced?

In peritoneal dialysis, catheters are surgically inserted into the abdominal cavity, through which the dialysate enters and leaves.

The process where substances are moved from the filtrate back into the blood is called:

Selective reabsorption is the process by which certain substances that have been filtered out of the blood are reabsorbed back into it.

Which of the following is NOT a cause of chronic renal failure?

Severe hemorrhage due to trauma is a cause of acute renal failure. Chronic renal failure may be caused by bacterial infections, high blood pressure, and diabetes mellitus.

The funnel-shaped structure within the kidney that collects urine before it passes into the ureter is the:

The ureter begins as the funnel-shaped renal pelvis, which collects urine within the kidney.

The descending limb of the loop of Henle is primarily permeable to \_\_\_\_\_\_, while the ascending limb actively transports \_\_\_\_\_\_.

Water passes out of the descending limb by osmosis, while the ascending limb actively transports sodium chloride out into the surrounding kidney interstitium.

Which structure carries urine from the urinary bladder to the outside of the body?

The urethra is a tube that carries urine from the urinary bladder to the outside of the body.

The main purpose of secreting hydrogen ions (H+) into the filtrate is to:

The main purpose of secreting substances like hydrogen ions is to maintain the pH of the urine, which normally ranges from 4.8 to 7.5.

A homeotherm is an animal that:

Homeotherms, like birds and mammals, are able to maintain a fairly constant body temperature by using physiological mechanisms.

All of the following are typically found in the glomerular filtrate EXCEPT:

Larger molecules like proteins, as well as red blood cells and platelets, are left behind in the blood and do not pass into the glomerular filtrate.

Hypercalciuria is a condition that directly contributes to the formation of which type of kidney stone?

Increased calcium in the blood (hypercalcemia) causes high calcium in the urine (hypercalciuria), which along with high oxalate levels (hyperoxaluria) causes calcium oxalate stones.

Which part of the renal tubule is responsible for reabsorbing the majority of water, ions, and all organic nutrients like glucose?

The proximal convoluted tubule is where the reabsorption of water, ions, and all organic nutrients occurs.

Shivering warms the body primarily through:

Shivering is the spasmodic contraction of muscles, and this contraction produces heat which helps to raise the body temperature.

What is the primary role of the urinary bladder?

The urinary bladder is a hollow and distensible muscular organ that serves as a urine reservoir.

An animal that excretes urea as its primary nitrogenous waste is described as:

Animals which excrete urea as their major nitrogenous waste product are called ureotelic.

The tough, fibrous connective tissue layer that encloses and protects each kidney is called the:

A fibrous connective tissue layer, called the fibrous capsule, encloses each kidney.

Besides excretion, the kidneys play a crucial osmoregulatory role by:

As osmoregulatory organs, the kidneys are concerned with the formation of diluted urine during flooding and concentrated urine during dehydration.

Which hormone, secreted by the posterior pituitary, promotes the reabsorption of water from the collecting ducts?

The movement of water out of the descending limb and collecting duct is promoted by anti-diuretic hormone (ADH), which is secreted from the posterior lobe of the pituitary.

The cone-shaped tissues within the renal medulla are called:

The renal medulla consists of a number of cone-shaped renal pyramids.

Piloerection helps to warm the body by:

Piloerection, or the 'erection of skin hair', traps air in the erected hair, which acts as an insulator for heat.

A key factor in a successful kidney transplant is matching the donor and recipient's ABO blood group and which other factor?

ABO blood group compatibility is essential for a kidney transplant, and it is also usual to select donor kidneys on the basis of human leucocytes antigen (HLA) matching to improve graft survival.

In humans, the small amount of uric acid excreted daily is primarily a breakdown product of:

Humans excrete small quantities of uric acid, but this is produced from the breakdown of nucleic acids, not proteins.

The final part of the nephron, which receives filtrate from several distal convoluted tubules, is the:

The distal convoluted tubule is connected to the collecting duct, which receives filtrate from many nephrons.

A patient with end-stage renal disease (ESRD) might have a condition called uremia, which is characterized by:

Chronic kidney failure can progress to end-stage renal disease (ESRD) and uremia, which is fatal unless treated with dialysis or a transplant.

Which of these is a behavioral, rather than physiological, response to thermoregulation?

A behavioral response, such as changing clothing, is voluntary and often corrects a temperature imbalance without the need for a physiological response like shivering or sweating.

The medial depression of the kidney where the renal artery, renal vein, and ureter connect is called the:

The medial side of the kidney is concave, and the entrance to the hollow chamber (renal sinus) is termed the hilum, where the renal artery and nerves enter, and the renal vein and ureter exit.

In peritoneal dialysis, the exchange of waste products occurs across which membrane?

Peritoneal dialysis involves the use of a natural membrane in the body, the peritoneum, which encloses the abdominal cavity.

The conversion of highly toxic ammonia to the less toxic urea occurs in which organ?

The liver supports the excretory role of the kidney by producing ammonia, urea, and uric acid from the nitrogen of amino acids.

Which of the following would be a cause of acute, rather than chronic, renal failure?

Acute renal failure may be caused by an obstruction of the ureters, bladder, or urethra by a kidney stone. Diabetes and high blood pressure are causes of chronic renal failure.

The primary function of the Loop of Henle is to \_\_\_\_\_\_ in the renal medulla.

The formation of hypertonic (concentrated) urine is dependent on the reabsorption of water from the loop of Henle and collecting duct, achieved by the countercurrent multiplier mechanism.

Vasodilation helps to cool the body by:

Vasodilation is the expansion of blood capillaries beneath the skin, which increases blood flow and allows heat to be carried away from the blood through the skin.

Urea is approximately how many times less toxic than ammonia?

Organisms invest energy to convert ammonia into urea, which is 100,000 times less toxic than ammonia.

The science concerned with the structure, function, and diseases of the kidney is called:

The science concerned with the structure, functions, and diseases of the kidneys is called nephrology.

Which hormone acts on the ascending limb of the loop of Henle to control the active transport of sodium chloride?

The movement of sodium chloride out of the ascending limb is controlled by the hormone aldosterone.

Which of the following best describes the path of filtrate through the nephron after leaving the Bowman's capsule?

The renal tubule leads away from the Bowman's capsule and becomes the proximal convoluted tubule (PCT), which then forms the loop of Henle, followed by the distal convoluted tubule (DCT), and finally connects to the collecting duct.

The PCNL procedure accesses a kidney stone:

In PCNL, a tube is inserted from the patient's back into the kidney to create a tract, through which a scope and equipment can be inserted to break up and remove the stone.

The main difference between poikilotherms and homeotherms is based on:

Animals are classified as poikilotherms if they are unable to maintain their body temperature within narrow limits, and as homeotherms if they are able to maintain a fairly constant body temperature.

If a person's kidneys were failing, what would you expect to happen to the concentration of urea in their blood?

Kidney failure is a decline in kidney function, particularly the efficiency of the filtering process. This leads to a buildup of nitrogenous wastes like urea in the blood.

The expansion of blood capillaries just beneath the skin to increase heat loss is known as:

Vasodilation is the expansion of blood capillaries which lie just beneath the epidermis of the skin, leading to more blood flow and subsequent heat loss.

A staghorn stone is a large kidney stone that is notable for its:

A branching stone that can fill the renal pelvis is called a staghorn stone.

Which of these animals would be classified as a homeothermic endotherm?

Homeotherms (maintaining constant temperature) include birds and mammals. Endotherms (producing their own heat) also include birds and mammals. Humans are mammals.
