## 1. 🩸 Blood Group ABO System [Appeared 6+ tx]

**CORE CONCEPT:**
- Blood group O → has NO antigens on RBCs → has BOTH Anti-A and Anti-B antibodies in plasma → Universal DONOR
- Blood group AB → has BOTH A and B antigens → has NO antibodies → Universal RECIPIENT
- AB × O cross → children can ONLY be A or B (never O, never AB)
- A × B (both hetero🔹ygous IAi × IBi) → children can be A, B, AB, or O (ratio 1:1:1:1)

**ALL VARIANTS HAS ASKED:**
| Variant | Years Asked | What They Want |
|---------|------------|----------------|
| "Why can AB receive from anyone?" | 2012 | No antibodies in plasma (NOT "no antigens"!) |
| "What does blood type O have?" | 2024 | Anti-A and Anti-B antibodies in PLASMA |
| "Mother AB × Father O → children?" | 2021, undated | ONLY A and B (never O!) |
| "Both parents hetero🔹ygous A × B?" | Undated | A, B, AB, O all possible (25% each) |
| "Girl has A, brother has B → parents?" | Undated | One parent must have IAIB or both are IAi × IBi |
| "Which group doesn't show codominance?" | 2023 | O (it has no antigens to co-express) |

**🪤 TRAP ANSWERS:** 
- "AB has neither A nor B ANTIGENS" ← WRONG. AB has BOTH antigens. It has neither A nor B ANTIBODIES.
- "AB × O → child can be O" ← WRONG. O needs ii genotype, but AB parent MUST give IA or IB.

**Answer: 100%** (Every child gets D from father → all Rh+)

**🪤 TRAP:** Students pick 50% thinking of hetero🔹ygous Dd father. Read carefully if father is HOMOZYGOUS (DD).

## 3. 🧫 Mendelian Genetics Crosses [Appeared 5+ tx]

**CORE CONCEPT:**
- Monohybrid Bb × bb → 50% dominant, 50% recessive
- Monohybrid Bb × Bb → 3:1 (phenotype), 1:2:1 (genotype)
- Dihybrid TtVv × ttvv → 1:1:1:1 (test cross ratio)

| Pedigree chart → deduce pattern | Autosomal recessive if skips generations |

**🪤 TRAP:** In dihybrid cross RRPp × rrpp, students expect 4 phenotypes. But one parent is RR (homo🔹ygous dominant), so ALL offspring are Rr (valley) → only the P gene segregates → 1:1 ratio, NOT 1:1:1:1.

## 6. ⚖️ Chemical Equilibrium Concepts [Appeared 4+ tx]

**CORE CONCEPT:**
- At equilibrium, reaction does NOT stop — forward and reverse rates are EQUAL
- Kc does NOT change when concentration changes (only changes with TEMPERATURE)
- Large Kc → reaction almost complete (products favored)
- Small Kc → reactants favored
- Adding catalyst does NOT change equilibrium position (only speeds up both rates equally)
- Equal moles on both sides → pressure change has NO effect

**ALL VARIANTS:**
| Variant | Answer |
|---------|--------|
| "Which is INCORRECT about equilibrium?" | "Reaction ceases at equilibrium" ← THIS IS WRONG |
| If [B] doubled, Kc will be? | UNAFFECTED (Kc is constant at fixed T) |
| Very large Kc (10⁷³) suggests? | Reaction is almost completed |
| Very small Kc suggests? | Proceeds very slowly to products |
| Equal moles both sides → pressure increase? | No effect / remains undisturbed |

**🪤 TRAP:** "Reaction ceases at equilibrium" — puts this as a correct-sounding option. It's ALWAYS wrong. Equilibrium is DYNAMIC = both reactions still occurring.

| Blue light through double slit → pattern? | Bands of black and blue |
| Increase fringe distance → what to do? | ↑L and ↓d (screen farther, slits closer) |

## 8. 🌡️ Thermodynamics (1st & 2nd Law) [Appeared 4+ tx]

**CORE CONCEPT:**
- 1st Law = conservation of ENERGY (ΔQ = ΔU + ΔW)
- Adiabatic: ΔQ=0 → ΔU = -ΔW
- Isochoric: ΔV=0 → ΔW=0, so ΔQ=ΔU (constant volume = line along V-axis)
- Isobaric: constant pressure
- 2nd Law: Heat flows from hot to cold; need TWO reservoirs at different T for work
- Carnot efficiency: η = 1 - Tc/Th (temperatures in KELVIN!)

**ALL VARIANTS:**
| Variant | Answer |
|---------|--------|
| 1st law explains conservation of? | Energy |
| Adiabatic equation (1st law)? | ΔU = -ΔW |
| Line parallel to V-axis = ? | Isochoric process |
| 2nd law: heat→work needs? | Two reservoirs at different T |
| Hot reservoir 427°C, cold 77°C → efficiency? | 50% (1 - 350/700) |
| Total energy of system = ? | KE + PE |

**🪤 TRAP:** Students forget to convert Celsius to Kelvin! 427°C = 700K, 77°C = 350K. Always add 273.

- Where does filtration occur? → Renal corpuscle
- Where is glucose reabsorbed? → PCT
- Why no glucose in urine? → Active transport from PCT
- Where is urea made? → Liver
- In dialysis, what's kept same as blood? → Glucose

- pH = pKa + log[salt]/[acid]

## 41. Exothermic Reactions

## 46. Unreacted Excess Reactant

## 47. Scalar vs Vector

## 48. Parallel Current-Carrying Conductors

### ➕ Physics: Variations in Weight (g)
- **Concept:** The acceleration due to gravity ($g$) and hence weight ($W$) is maximum at sea level. It decreases when going deep into a mine (due to less mass pulling inwards) and decreases when climbing a mountain (due to increased distance from the center).
- **MCQ Application:** Weight at sea level ($W_S$) > Weight in coal mine ($W_C$) and Weight at sea level ($W_S$) > Weight on mountain ($W_M$). You will be asked to compare them.

### ➕ Chemistry: Semiconductors & Temperature
- **Concept:** Elements like Germanium (Ge) and Silicon (Si) are metalloids. At low temperatures, they are poor conductors. As temperature increases, electrons jump to the conduction band, *increasing* their conductivity. This is the opposite of metals!

### ➕ Biology: Photoperiodism & Reproduction
- **Concept:** Short-day plants require an uninterrupted period of darkness to flower. If this dark period is disturbed by even a flash of light, they will fail to flower and may resort to **vegetative reproduction**.

### ➕ Biology: En🔹yme Kinetics & Inhibition Traps
- **Competitive Inhibitors:** Bind at the active site. They **increase** $K_m$ (decrease affinity) but leave $V_{max}$ **unchanged** (can be overcome by adding more substrate). loves testing the graph—they cross the y-axis at the same point!
- **Non-Competitive Inhibitors:** Bind at the allosteric site. They leave $K_m$ **unchanged** but **decrease** $V_{max}$. Adding more substrate does *not* overcome this.

### ➕ Biology: Genetics & Pedigree Mastery Rules
- **Autosomal Dominant:** NEVER skips a generation. Affected individuals have at least one affected parent.
- **X-Linked Recessive:** Males are affected much more. An affected mother **always** passes it to ALL her sons. tests this specific scenario repeatedly to check if you know males cannot be carriers.
- **Mitochondrial Inheritance:** An affected mother passes it to **100% of her children**. An affected father passes it to **0%**.

### ➕ Biology: The Counter-Current Multiplier 
- **Descending Loop of Henle is highly permeable to WATER, but NOT salts.** Filtrate becomes hypertonic (concentrated) as water leaves.
- **Ascending Loop of Henle is permeable to SALTS (Na+, Cl- actively pumped out), but completely IMPERMEABLE to water.** Filtrate becomes hypotonic (dilute). will swap these to trick you.

### ➕ Chemistry: Electrophilic Substitution on Ben🔹ene
- **Ortho/Para Directors (Activating):** -OH, -NH2, -CH3, Halogens (Halogens are the famous exception: they are *deactivating* but still *ortho/para directing*!).
- **Meta Directors (Deactivating):** -NO2, -COOH, -SO3H, -CHO. Any substituent with a double/triple bond directly attached to the ring is meta-directing.

### ➕ Chemistry: Le Chatelier's & Industrial Conditions
- **Haber Process (Ammonia):** Exothermic (Exothermic, delta H is negative), meaning high yield occurs at **LOW temperatures**. However, a *compromise* temperature (450°C) is used so the reaction isn't agoni🔹ingly slow. Catalyst = Iron.
- **Contact Process (Sulfuric Acid):** Also exothermic. V2O5 catalyst used at 450°C. Adding pressure pushes equilibrium to the side with *fewer* moles of gas. 

### ➕ Physics: The Doppler Effect Scenarios
- **Observer moving towards stationary source:** Apparent frequency increases because relative velocity of waves *increases*.
- **Source moving towards stationary observer:** Apparent frequency increases because apparent wavelength *decreases*. Be extremely careful knowing *why* the frequency changes.

### ➕ Physics: Projectile Motion "Maximi🔹ation"
- **Max Range:** Angle is 45°. At 45°, Height = 1/4 of Range.
- **Same Range Angles:** Any two angles that add up to 90° (e.g., 30° and 60°, or 15° and 75°) will give the **exact same range** but different heights and tx of flight.

### 🔹 Biology: Antibody Structures
- **Concept:** Antibodies (Immunoglobulins) have heavy and light chains connected by disulfide bonds.
- **Application:** **IgG** is the most abundant and crosses the placenta. **IgM** is the pentamer (largest, first to respond). **IgA** is found in secretions (tears, breast milk). **IgE** responds to allergies and parasites.
