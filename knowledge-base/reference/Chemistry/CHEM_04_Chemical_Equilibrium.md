## CHEM-04 — Chemical Equilibrium

---

⭐ **Q119.** The direction of a chemical reaction can be predicted by means of its ratio. When this ratio is less than Kc, the reaction proceeds to:
- A. Forward direction
- B. Backward direction
- C. At equilibrium
**Answer: A** [Official Sample]

---

⭐ **Q120.** Which is CORRECT for the reaction: 2O₃ ⇌ 3O₂, Kc = 10⁵⁵ at 25°C?
- A. Increase in pressure increases rate of forward reaction
- B. Ozone formation is exothermic
- C. Ozone is highly unstable at room temperature
**Answer: C** [Official Sample]

---

**Q121.** [ 2012] The value of Kc is 10⁷³. This suggests the reaction:
- A. Is at equilibrium
- B. Does not proceed to products
- C. Proceeds to reactant
- D. Is almost completed
**Answer: D**

---

**Q122.** [ 2012] If concentration of B is doubled in A + B ⇌ 2C, the value of Kc will be:
- A. Halved
- B. Doubled
- C. Trebled
- D. Unaffected
**Answer: D**

---

🔥 **Q123.** [ 2024] [REPEATED 3x: 2022, 2024 both] Which statement is INCORRECT regarding equilibrium?
- A. The reaction ceases at equilibrium
- B. Adding catalyst doesn't change equilibrium
- C. Can be approached from either side
**Answer: A** (Reaction does NOT cease — forward and reverse rates are equal)
