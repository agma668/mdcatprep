## 🎓 KNOWN TEST PATTERNS (From Research)

### **Test Structure:**
The test is divided into two sections:
1. Science Achievement section: 50 MCQs
2. Science & Mathematics Reasoning section: 50 MCQs
Total: 100 MCQs in 2 hours

### **Negative Marking:**
- Biology, Physics, Chemistry: **-0.25 marks** per wrong answer
- Mathematics Reasoning, Science Reasoning: **No negative marking**

### **High-Yield Topics (Based on Past Paper Analysis):**

**BIOLOGY (35 MCQs expected):**
- Genetics & Inheritance (pedigree, dominance patterns)
- Cell Biology (organelles, cell cycle, mitosis/meiosis)
- Human Physiology (especially excretion, circulation, endocrine)
- Enzymes (mechanism, inhibition, kinetics)
- Biotechnology (PCR, DNA replication, gene therapy)

**CHEMISTRY (35 MCQs expected):**
- Chemical Equilibrium (Kc, Kp, Le Chatelier)
- Organic Chemistry (reactions, mechanisms, benzene)
- Thermochemistry (enthalpy, Hess's law)
- Acids/Bases/Buffers (pH calculations, Henderson-Hasselbalch)
- Electrochemistry (redox, cell potential)

**PHYSICS (30 MCQs expected):**
- Electromagnetism (magnetic force, induction, flux)
- Current Electricity (Ohm's law, circuits, power)
- Thermodynamics (laws, processes, heat engines)
- Modern Physics (nuclear reactions, spectra, photoelectric)
- Optics (wave optics, interference, diffraction)

**MATHEMATICS REASONING (20 MCQs expected):**
- Percentages & Proportions
- Algebra (equations, inequalities)
- Geometry & Mensuration
- Logic & Set Theory
- Probability & Statistics

**SCIENCE REASONING (15 MCQs expected):**
- Biology passages (enzyme mechanisms, blood groups, growth)
- Chemistry passages (electrolysis, gas laws, crystals)
- Physics passages (circuits, gears, mechanics)
