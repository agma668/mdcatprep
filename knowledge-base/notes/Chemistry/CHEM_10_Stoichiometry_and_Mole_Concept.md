⭐ **Q150.** What is the correct percentage of Na in NaOH? (Mol. wt = 40g)
- A. 40%
- B. 57.5%
- C. 59.5%
**Answer: B** [Official Sample] (23/40 × 100 = 57.5%)

---

⭐ **Q151.** When 4g of NaOH is dissolved in 1L, the concentration is:
- A. 1M
- B. 0.5M
- C. 0.25M
**Answer: C** [Official Sample] (4/40 = 0.1 mol/L = 0.1M — but given options suggest D=0.1M may be missing)
