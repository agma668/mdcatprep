## Chapter 7, 8, 9: Hydrocarbons & Benzene (Crucial Additions)

### Preparation of Alkanes
- **Wurtz Synthesis (Preparation of Symmetrical Alkanes)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">2CH3-Cl + 2Na ⟶ CH3-CH3 + 2NaCl</span><br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Ethane | IUPAC: Ethane</span>
- **Decarboxylation of Sodium Salts of Fatty Acids**
 <span class="reaction-eq">CH3-COONa + NaOH ⟶ CH4 + Na2CO3</span> (CaO, Δ)<br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Methane (Marsh Gas) | IUPAC: Methane</span>
- **Sabatier-Senderens Reaction (Hydrogenation)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">CH2=CH2 + H2 ⟶ CH3-CH3</span> (Ni, 250°C)<br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Ethylene ⟶ Ethane | IUPAC: Ethene ⟶ Ethane</span>

### Preparation of Alkenes & Alkynes
- **Dehydrohalogenation of Alkyl Halides (Alkene Prep)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">CH3-CH2-Cl + KOH(alc) ⟶ CH2=CH2 + KCl + H2O</span><br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Ethyl Chloride ⟶ Ethylene | IUPAC: Chloroethane ⟶ Ethene</span>
- **Dehalogenation of Vicinal Dihalides (Alkene Prep)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">CH2(Br)-CH2(Br) + Zn ⟶ CH2=CH2 + ZnBr2</span><br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Ethylene dibromide ⟶ Ethylene | IUPAC: 1,2-Dibromoethane ⟶ Ethene</span>
- **Preparation of Alkynes (From Calcium Carbide)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">CaC2 + 2H2O ⟶ HC≡CH + Ca(OH)2</span><br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Acetylene | IUPAC: Ethyne</span>
- **Dehydrohalogenation of Vicinal Dihalides (Alkyne Prep)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">CH2(Br)-CH2(Br) + 2KOH(alc) ⟶ HC≡CH + 2KBr + 2H2O</span>

### Important Hydrocarbon Reactions
- **Ozonolysis of Alkenes (Cleavage)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">CH2=CH2 + O3 ⟶ [Ozonide] ⟶ 2HCHO + H2O2</span> (Zn/H2O)<br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Formaldehyde | IUPAC: Methanal</span>
- **Hydration of Alkynes (Aldehyde/Ketone Prep)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">HC≡CH + H2O ⟶ [Vinyl Alcohol] ⟶ CH3CHO</span> (HgSO4/H2SO4)<br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Acetaldehyde | IUPAC: Ethanal</span>

### Benzene & Electrophilic Aromatic Substitution (EAS)
- **Preparation from Phenol**
 <span class="reaction-eq">C6H5OH + Zn ⟶ C6H6 + ZnO</span><br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Benzene | IUPAC: Benzene</span>
- **Polymerization of Acetylene to Benzene**
 <span class="reaction-eq">3HC≡CH ⟶ C6H6</span> (Cu tube, 300°C)
- **Friedel-Crafts Alkylation**
 <span class="reaction-eq">C6H6 + CH3Cl ⟶ C6H5-CH3 + HCl</span> (AlCl3)<br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Toluene | IUPAC: Methylbenzene</span>
- **Friedel-Crafts Acylation**
 <span class="reaction-eq">C6H6 + CH3COCl ⟶ C6H5-CO-CH3 + HCl</span> (AlCl3)<br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Acetophenone | IUPAC: 1-Phenylethanone</span>
- **Nitration of Benzene**
 <span class="reaction-eq">C6H6 + HNO3 ⟶ C6H5-NO2 + H2O</span> (conc. H2SO4, 50°C)<br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Nitrobenzene | IUPAC: Nitrobenzene</span>

## Chapter 10: Alkyl Halides

### Alkyl Halide Preparation (from Alcohols)
- **Reaction with Thionyl Chloride (SOCl2 - Darzen's Process)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">R-OH + SOCl2 ⟶ R-Cl + SO2 + HCl</span>
- **Reaction with Phosphorus Trihalide & Pentachloride**
 <span class="reaction-eq">3R-OH + PX3 ⟶ 3R-X + H3PO3</span><br>
 <span class="reaction-eq">R-OH + PCl5 ⟶ R-Cl + POCl3 + HCl</span>

### Reactions of Alkyl Halides
- **Nucleophilic Substitution (Alcohol Prep)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">R-X + KOH(aq) ⟶ R-OH + KX</span>
- **Williamson Ether Synthesis**
 <span class="reaction-eq">C2H5ONa + C2H5Br ⟶ C2H5-O-C2H5 + NaBr</span><br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Diethyl Ether | IUPAC: Ethoxyethane</span>
- **Formation of Nitriles/Cyanides (Chain Lengthening)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">CH3-Cl + KCN ⟶ CH3-CN + KCl</span><br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Methyl Cyanide | IUPAC: Ethanenitrile</span>

### Grignard Reagent (R-Mg-X) Reactions
- **Formation of Grignard Reagent**
 <span class="reaction-eq">CH3-CH2-Br + Mg ⟶ CH3-CH2-Mg-Br</span> (in dry ether)<br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Ethylmagnesium Bromide</span>
- **Reaction with Formaldehyde (1° Alcohol Prep)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">CH3-Mg-Br + HCHO ⟶ ⟶ CH3-CH2-OH</span><br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Ethyl Alcohol | IUPAC: Ethanol</span>
- **Reaction with Acetaldehyde (2° Alcohol Prep)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">CH3-Mg-Br + CH3CHO ⟶ ⟶ CH3-CH(OH)-CH3</span><br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Isopropyl Alcohol | IUPAC: Propan-2-ol</span>
- **Reaction with Acetone (3° Alcohol Prep)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">CH3-Mg-Br + CH3COCH3 ⟶ ⟶ (CH3)3C-OH</span><br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: tert-Butyl Alcohol | IUPAC: 2-Methylpropan-2-ol</span>
- **Reaction with Carbon Dioxide (Carboxylic Acid Prep)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">CH3-Mg-Br + CO2 ⟶ ⟶ CH3-COOH</span><br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Acetic Acid | IUPAC: Ethanoic Acid</span>

## Chapter 11: Alcohols, Phenols & Ethers

### Alcohols
- **Industrial Preparation of Methanol**
 <span class="reaction-eq">CO + 2H2 ⟶ CH3OH</span> (ZnO/Cr2O3, 400°C, 200 atm)<br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Wood Spirit | IUPAC: Methanol</span>
- **Fermentation of Starch to Ethanol**
 <span class="reaction-eq">C12H22O11 + H2O ⟶ 4C2H5OH + 4CO2</span> (Invertase, Zymase)<br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Grain Alcohol | IUPAC: Ethanol</span>
- **Oxidation of Primary Alcohols (Aldehyde Prep)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">CH3CH2OH + [O] ⟶ CH3CHO + H2O</span> (K2Cr2O7 / H2SO4)
- **High-Temp Dehydration (Alkene Prep)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">C2H5OH ⟶ CH2=CH2 + H2O</span> (H2SO4, 180°C)
- **Low-Temp Dehydration (Ether Prep)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">2C2H5OH ⟶ C2H5-O-C2H5 + H2O</span> (H2SO4, 140°C)

### Phenols
- **Dow's Process (Phenol Prep from Chlorobenzene)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">C6H5Cl + 2NaOH ⟶ C6H5ONa ⟶ C6H5OH</span> (360°C, 150 atm)
- **Nitration (Preparation of Picric Acid)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">C6H5OH + 3HNO3 ⟶ 2,4,6-Trinitrophenol + 3H2O</span>
- **Bakelite Polymer Formation**
 <span class="reaction-eq">Phenol + Formaldehyde ⟶ o/p-Hydroxybenzyl alcohol ⟶ Bakelite</span>

### Ethers
- **Cleavage by Hydroiodic Acid (HI)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">C2H5-O-C2H5 + HI ⟶ C2H5I + C2H5OH</span>

- **Preparation of Acetone (Dry Distillation of Calcium Acetate)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">(CH3COO)2Ca ⟶ CH3COCH3 + CaCO3</span> (Heat)<br>

- **Aldol Condensation**
 <span class="reaction-eq">2CH3CHO ⟶ CH3-CH(OH)-CH2-CHO</span> (dil. NaOH)<br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Aldol | IUPAC: 3-Hydroxybutanal</span>
- **Cannizzaro Reaction (Formaldehyde)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">2HCHO + NaOH ⟶ CH3OH + HCOONa</span><br>
 <span class="chem-names">Products: Methanol + Sodium Formate</span>
- **Cannizzaro Reaction (Benzaldehyde)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">2C6H5CHO + NaOH ⟶ C6H5CH2OH + C6H5COONa</span><br>
 <span class="chem-names">Products: Benzyl Alcohol + Sodium Benzoate</span>
- **Iodoform Test for Acetone/Acetaldehyde**
 <span class="reaction-eq">CH3COCH3 + 3I2 + 4NaOH ⟶ CHI3 + CH3COONa + 3NaI + 3H2O</span><br>
 <span class="chem-names">Product: Iodoform (Yellow ppt)</span>

## Chapter 13: Carboxylic Acids

### Preparation Methods
- **From Primary Alcohols (Oxidation)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">R-CH2-OH + 2[O] ⟶ R-COOH + H2O</span> (K2Cr2O7 / H2SO4)
- **Hydrolysis of Nitriles**
 <span class="reaction-eq">CH3-CN + 2H2O ⟶ CH3-COOH + NH3</span> (H+ catalyzed)

### Chemical Reactions
- **Esterification**
 <span class="reaction-eq">CH3COOH + C2H5OH ⟶ CH3COOC2H5 + H2O</span> (H2SO4 catalyst)<br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Ethyl Acetate | IUPAC: Ethyl Ethanoate</span>
- **Formation of Acid Chlorides**
 <span class="reaction-eq">CH3COOH + PCl5 ⟶ CH3COCl + POCl3 + HCl</span><br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Acetyl Chloride | IUPAC: Ethanoyl Chloride</span>
- **Hell-Volhard-Zelinsky (HVZ) Reaction**
 <span class="reaction-eq">CH3COOH + Br2 ⟶ Br-CH2-COOH + HBr</span> (Red P catalyst)<br>
 <span class="chem-names">Common: Bromoacetic Acid | IUPAC: 2-Bromoethanoic Acid</span>
- **Strecker Synthesis (Amino Acid Prep)**
 <span class="reaction-eq">R-CHO + HCN + NH3 ⟶ R-CH(NH2)CN ⟶ R-CH(NH2)COOH</span>

## High-Yield Summary Tables & Concepts (Exam Extras)

### 1. Functional Group Identification Tests
| Test Name | Reagent | Positive Result (Observation) | Compounds that test Positive |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lucas Test** | ZnCl2 + conc. HCl | Cloudiness/Turbidity | 3° (immediate), 2° (5 mins), 1° (no reaction at RT) |
| **Iodoform Test** | I2 + NaOH | Yellow ppt (CHI3) | Compounds with CH3-CO- or CH3-CH(OH)- groups (e.g., Ethanol, Acetaldehyde, Acetone) |
| **Tollen's Test** | Ammoniacal AgNO3 | Silver Mirror | All Aldehydes (Aliphatic & Aromatic) |
| **Fehling's/Benedict's** | Cu(OH)2 + NaOH / Citrate | Red ppt (Cu2O) | Aliphatic Aldehydes only (Not Ketones or Benzaldehyde) |
| **2,4-DNPH Test** | 2,4-Dinitrophenylhydrazine | Yellow/Orange ppt | All Aldehydes & Ketones |
| **Baeyer's Test** | Cold, dil. alkaline KMnO4 | Purple color decolorizes | Alkenes and Alkynes (Unsaturation) |
| **Bromine Water** | Br2 in H2O/CCl4 | Orange-red color decolorizes | Alkenes, Alkynes, Phenol (gives white ppt) |

### 2. Crucial Reaction Rules & Mechanisms
- **Markovnikov's Rule:** In the addition of HX to an asymmetrical alkene, the hydrogen attaches to the carbon with more hydrogens.
- **Anti-Markovnikov's Rule (Peroxide Effect):** In the presence of organic peroxides, addition of **HBr** (only HBr) to asymmetrical alkenes occurs contrary to Markovnikov's rule.
- **Zaitsev's Rule:** During dehydrohalogenation (elimination), the more substituted (more stable) alkene is the major product.
- **Electrophilic Aromatic Substitution (EAS):** The characteristic reaction of Benzene. The attacking species is an electrophile ($E^+$).
 - *Nitration Electrophile:* $NO_2^+$ (Nitronium ion)
 - *Sulfonation Electrophile:* $SO_3$ (Sulfur trioxide)
 - *Halogenation Electrophile:* $Cl^+$ / $Br^+$ (Halonium ion)
 - *Alkylation Electrophile:* $R^+$ (Carbocation)
- **Directing Groups in Benzene:**
 - **Ortho/Para Directors (Activating):** -OH, -NH2, -CH3, -OCH3 (Exceptions: Halogens -Cl, -Br are o/p directors but *deactivating*).
 - **Meta Directors (Deactivating):** -NO2, -COOH, -CHO, -CN, -SO3H.

### 3. Acidic & Basic Trends (Frequently Tested)
- **Acidity Order:** Carboxylic Acid > Phenol > Water > Alcohol > Terminal Alkyne
- **Reactivity towards Nucleophilic Addition (Aldehydes/Ketones):** Formaldehyde > Acetaldehyde > Acetone > Diethyl Ketone
- **Reactivity of Alkyl Halides (SN1):** 3° > 2° > 1° > CH3X *(Favors tertiary, weak nucleophile, polar protic solvent, racemic mixture)*
- **Reactivity of Alkyl Halides (SN2):** CH3X > 1° > 2° > 3° *(Favors primary, strong nucleophile, polar aprotic solvent, Walden inversion)*
- **Leaving Group Ability:** I⁻ > Br⁻ > Cl⁻ > F⁻
- **Boiling Point Order:** Amides > Carboxylic Acids > Alcohols > Ketones > Aldehydes > Amines > Esters > Ethers > Alkanes (Assuming comparable molecular weights).

### 4. Important Catalysts
- **Lindlar's Catalyst (Pd/BaSO4 + Quinoline):** Reduces alkynes to **Cis-Alkenes**.
- **Na/Liquid NH3 (Birch Reduction):** Reduces alkynes to **Trans-Alkenes**.
- **Ziegler-Natta Catalyst (TiCl4 + (C2H5)3Al):** Polymerization of ethene to high-density polyethene.
- **Adam's Catalyst (PtO2):** Hydrogenation catalyst.
- **V2O5 (Vanadium Pentoxide):** Oxidation of benzene to maleic anhydride, or SO2 to SO3 in Contact Process.
